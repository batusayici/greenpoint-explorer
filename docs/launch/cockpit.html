Rules of play
  • Max 3 live experiments. Attribution dies past that.
  • Every experiment ships with hypothesis → smallest test → one metric → decision rule → kill criteria written before launch. No kill criteria, no launch.
  • Pre/post and small-n qualitative only. No A/B infrastructure, no significance theater — at this traffic it's noise cosplay.
  • September reads carry the autumn-rebound confound and nothing graduates on a Sep–Oct read alone. Carve-out: contemporaneous-control designs (R1, Q2) are exempt — 2a governs pre/post reads.
  • Each Q experiment closes at the readout after its rule fires, or after 4 weeks, whichever comes first. No experiment squats on a slot.
  • The unit of seeding is a small trust network, not reach. Saturate one network before opening the next.
  • Batu sends every outbound message. Every link is copied from channel-links.md, never composed by hand.
  • Paid acquisition is dead permanently, not deferred — revenue per user is $0, so only near-zero-CAC channels can ever fit.
  • Coverage is never for sale. Every verified business is on the map free.
  • P7: if R1 hasn't armed by the Mon Oct 5 readout, the next free slot goes to a no-list re-entry mechanism (R4 or per-lens .ics), and the late-Oct demand read is labeled 'measured without a re-entry mechanism' — a miss under it is never read as product failure alone.
  • P8: a demand-gate pass on cohorts wholly inside Sep–Oct is provisional until a Nov-or-later cohort clears the same bar — or it's accepted explicitly as a peak-season pass and disclosed in pilot conversations. Counterpart to the Dec–Feb no-read rule.
  • P9: the approved IG carousel test starts automatically at the first readout on/after Tue Oct 6 if tagged sessions across all sent srcs are under 50 (earlier by normal proposal when a slot frees). The medium question — will Greenpoint open a browser? — can no longer go unread.
  • P10: citations and AI-referrer sessions get their own readout lines. Citations up with sessions flat = Loop C working zero-click — H5's fail branch can't fire on it. llms.txt stays up, zero value booked.
  • P11: when Q1 closes, the freed slot goes to the arts/culture wave — Film Noir first, then WORD and Flower Cat. Sequencing only; never two networks at once.
