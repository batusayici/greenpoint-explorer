### The one metric this cannot touch — and why that matters

**The WRL proxy is structurally immune.** It counts `return_visit` with
`weekIndex ≥ 1`, which requires `localStorage` to survive a week. A stateless
fetcher can never produce it: all 85 first-visit events in the pull carry
`visitCount = 1`. Today's WRL proxy is **2 distinct people, 5 events (8/2 →
8/9)** — up from 1, against a bar of ≥30 in ≥3 of any 4 weeks.

So **the demand gate's own metric is clean; the activation and conversion
proxies are not.** That is worth stating plainly because it bounds how much this
finding actually costs us.

### What I am explicitly **not** proposing

**No retro-clean.** Batu ruled on 2026-08-06 that the funnel clock starts at
launch communication, that pre-Wave-1 rates are directional only, and that a
permanent person-ID exclusion list is the more expensive call. That ruling
covers this case exactly, and I am not re-litigating it — I am reporting the
numbers as the pull produces them, with the split shown alongside as a
diagnostic. **Wave 1 is the actual fix:** once tagged traffic arrives, every rate
that matters reads per-`src`, and untagged automated noise stops touching it.

**Likeliest cause, stated with its alternative.** The Stoopwise rename landed
8/6 and the first swarm arrived 8/7 — a new domain plus a sitemap, RSS, ICS and
`llms.txt` is an invitation to every scanner on the internet. The alternative
reading is that some of this is AI-assistant page fetching, which would be Loop C
working rather than noise. **The data cannot separate them**, and the distinction
does not change the recommendation: neither is a human in the activation
denominator.

  yield. Every one of those numbers is the highest ever recorded.
- **Smallest test:** the Wave 1 already specified in launch-plan §3 — three Q1
  org notes and one Q2 parents post, drafts below, links copied from
  `channel-links.md`. Nothing new is built.
- **Metric:** sessions and activation rate per `src`; `src=parents` week-2
  return.
- **Decision rule:** already pre-registered per experiment — Q1 "kill an
  org-type after 2 non-responses"; Q2 "double down only if return beats other
  `src`s." Neither is re-derived here.
- **Kill criteria:** n/a — sequencing, not an experiment. The experiments it
  starts carry their own.
- **Why this is the top proposal:** it is also the fix for Finding 3. Untagged
  automated traffic can only distort metrics that have no `src` to read
  against. The moment tagged traffic arrives, the numbers that matter become
  bot-proof by construction.
- **One judgment call flagged, not taken:** the deck is at a peak *because* the

  another week — but "next week" has now been the answer for three cycles.

### 2. Retire the all-history untagged activation rate as a headline number

- **Hypothesis:** publishing "activation 9%" as a headline is now actively
  misleading, and no amount of caveat text fixes it. The number fell 18% → 9%
  in a week while its numerator *rose* 7 → 9. It is a ratio of a real numerator
  to a denominator that is 60% machines. Batu already ruled that pre-Wave-1
  rates are directional only; this proposal is that the readout's *format*
  should stop contradicting that ruling.
- **Smallest test:** no code, no exclusion list, no retro-clean. From Wave 1
  onward the readout leads with **per-`src` activation** and reports the
  all-history untagged rate only as a footnote labelled "mixed population,
  directional." Until Wave 1 lands, report both columns as this readout does.
- **Metric:** whether a reader of the readout can state, unprompted, which
  population a headline rate describes.
- **Decision rule:** if per-`src` cohorts stay under ~10 people each for two
  cycles after Wave 1, they are too thin to lead with and the NYC-metro split
  becomes the reporting default instead.
- **Kill criteria:** n/a — a reporting rule.
- **Explicitly rejected alternative:** adding a bot-exclusion filter to
  `posthog-pull.sh`. That is the retro-clean Batu declined on 2026-08-06, it
  needs permanent maintenance, and geo/UA heuristics would themselves become a
  number nobody can audit. **Not proposed, and I would argue against it if
  raised.**
