## 🟢 Finding 1 — Search Console is live, and it says people find us by name, never by question

The two environment variables cycle 7 and cycle 8 asked for are in place. The
first read, for the week 8/30–9/5 against the week before:

| Search | 8/30–9/5 | 8/23–8/29 |
|---|---|---|
| Clicks | **20** | 8 (+150%) |
| Impressions | **425** | 248 (+71%) |
| CTR | **4.7%** | 3.2% |
| Average position | **10.5** | 13.7 (lower is better) |

**Every query is a lookup, not a question.** Thirteen queries produced
impressions. The two that produced clicks are `sereneco fire` (3 clicks, 65
impressions, position 7) and `kirbees greenpoint` (3 clicks, 51 impressions,
position 12). The rest are addresses, venue names and one event name —
`bedford slip`, `troost greenpoint`, `film noir cinema`, `211 meserole ave
brooklyn ny`. **Zero generic-intent queries**: nobody reached us by typing
"things to do in Greenpoint this weekend" or anything like it.

That is the question `L2026-08-17` left open, and this is the first window
that can answer it. It cuts both ways and both are worth saying plainly:

- **Against Loop C's original story.** The loop was drawn as answer engines
  fielding "what's happening in Greenpoint." Google is not sending us that
  traffic. It sends us people who already have a name in their head.
- **For the corpus.** Being the page that answers "what happened at Sereneco"
  is a real position — it is the reason the fire card outranks the local
  papers. Position improved by three places in a week and impressions rose
  71% while the deck grew, which is what an accruing corpus looks like.

**The pages that carry it are news cards.** `/e/sereneco-franklin-st-fire` (8
clicks, 151 impressions) and `/e/kirbees-opening` (4 clicks, 86) are 12 of the
20 clicks. News is the lens with 33 cards and **zero dated items, ever** — the
one we have been describing as the lens that carries no time-sensitive value.
It is our best-performing surface in search. No high-impression zero-click
pages this window.

**Recommendation (Batu's call):** nothing to decide, but two things follow.
The search half of the monthly citation check is now a script, so the manual
part shrinks to asking assistants directly (see Metrics, citations row). And
**the generic-intent finding belongs in the learning log against Q1** — an
entry is included in this PR.
