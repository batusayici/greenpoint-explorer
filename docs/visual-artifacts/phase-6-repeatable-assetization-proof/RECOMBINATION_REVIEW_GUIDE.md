# Recombination Review Guide

Status: Review guide for Batu / ChatGPT critique

Score each criterion from 1 to 5.

1 means the artifact fails the decision need. 5 means it is strong enough to justify deeper production-pipeline planning, subject to later approvals.

## Criteria

| criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Style fidelity | Drifts from approved Inked Indie / Compact Corner | Mostly aligned, with visible drift or weak finish | Clearly aligned with approved corpus in linework, palette, hatching, and mood |
| Modular repeatability | Looks bespoke or one-off only | Some shared parts are visible | Shared shells, signs, awnings, props, markers, and cards are visibly reusable |
| Storefront specificity | Generic storefront or asset-pack read | Some local texture and identity | Each storefront has distinct material, signage, props, and display rhythm |
| UI integration | UI floats separately or becomes QA harness | UI attaches but hierarchy needs tuning | Marker, outline, card, tether, controls, and index feel product-facing and art-integrated |
| Density / readability | Too sparse, too noisy, or illegible | Readable with some pressure points | Dense but scannable at map scale and close crop |
| Fictional-safe compliance | Real names, addresses, factual copy, or exact claims appear | Mostly fictional but some generated micro-text is risky | Fictional-safe labels only; no real addresses, factual claims, or exact map claims |
| Production-system promise | Does not imply reusable production path | Suggests promise but needs more proof | Strong enough to justify later production-pipeline planning |
| Drift risk | Drifts toward Phase 5.2 beige QA, SVG-grade, cozy fantasy, or generic vector | Minor drift to flag | Clear guardrails against known rejected directions |

## Suggested Review Flow

1. Review `module-sheet-v1.png` first for the shared part vocabulary.
2. Review `storefront-recombination-a.png`, `storefront-recombination-b.png`, and `storefront-recombination-c.png` for archetype breadth.
3. Review `street-slice-recombination-v1.png` for coexistence and scale.
4. Review `ui-integrated-recombination-v1.png` for selected state, marker, tether, card, compact controls, and place-index pressure.
5. Compare any concerns against `SYSTEM_MAP.md`, `EXTRACTION_RULES.md`, and `ASSET_KIT_MANIFEST.md`.

## Decision Questions

- Is the approved style repeatable without losing storefront specificity?
- Are the three archetypes materially different enough?
- Does the multi-module street slice still feel like the approved Greenpoint isometric direction?
- Does the UI/card/marker layer feel reusable and product-facing?
- Is this strong enough to plan a later production asset pipeline, or does another proof pass need to fix repeatability, fidelity, or UI drift first?
