## Takeaways Before Direction Selection

- OCCII pushes the project toward bolder social/flyer confidence, but its giant typography should not be copied into the explorable map surface.
- Classic sim/tycoon references support isometric readability and ambient detail, but not full game systems.
- eBoy and Habbo validate dense pixel worlds, but both can become too system-heavy or too interior/social.
- Riso/sticker references are strongest when used as UI/card/surface accents, not when they take over the architecture.
- Local storefront references must lead the world layer; otherwise the map becomes a generic creative-neighborhood toy.
- Map-symbol references are useful as a legibility discipline against overly decorative markers.
