Label: **generated development-frame review guide / not final approval**

## What This Review Should Decide

After the Batch 13 prompts are rendered, the review should decide:

- Does Soft Pixel remain strong once UI/card systems are introduced?
- Does Inked Indie remain usable once UI/card systems are introduced?
- Which direction has better product survivability?
- Which direction has better taste/locality balance?
- Should one direction advance to prototype visual-system exploration?

This review should not approve final art direction, production assets, app implementation, or real-place representation.

## Review Caveats

- Generated storefront names, addresses, labels, and button text are placeholder texture, not final copy.
- Some frames include more explicit map controls or legends than the prompts requested; judge whether that helps or hurts product clarity.
- Do not treat any fictional storefront, sign, address, or adjacency as a real-world claim.

## What To Judge

### 1. Clickability

Can a user see what is clickable before reading any explanation?

Look for entrances, facade boundaries, marker placement, hover state, selected state, and card connection.

### 2. Map Readability

Does the image still read as a browsable exterior isometric map?

Watch for texture, linework, pixel detail, or UI elements that flatten the scene or hide spatial logic.

### 3. UI Native-Ness

Do marker, hover, selected state, and card feel designed for the world?

Strong: UI is clearer than the world but materially related to it. Weak: UI feels pasted on, generic, or decorative.

### 4. Local Specificity

Does the direction still feel Greenpoint/Brooklyn-specific after product constraints are applied?

Look for facades, awnings, roll gates, bikes, stickered poles, planters, community boards, independent storefront signs, sidewalks, curbs, and street furniture.

### 5. Taste Level

Does the direction feel authored and culturally awake without drifting into generic genre language?

Soft Pixel should not become generic retro cozy. Inked Indie should not become generic comic/noir city.

### 6. Repeatability

Can this become a system for many storefronts and map states?

Look for modular facade logic, repeatable UI rules, density constraints, and clear asset categories.

### 7. Production Cost

Does the direction look plausible for MVP scope?

Strong: clear reusable modules and controlled detail. Weak: every storefront appears to require one-off hero art.

## Lightweight Scoring

Use 1 to 5.

- 1 = fails the criterion.
- 3 = promising but unresolved.
- 5 = strong enough to advance.

## What Not To Over-Index On

- Exact generated text.
- Exact business names.
- Exact real-world adjacency or placement.
- Small image-generation artifacts.
- Final typography.
- Final palette.
- Full app chrome, because this batch intentionally avoids it.

## Decision Output

A useful review decision should say one of:

- Advance Soft Pixel to prototype visual-system exploration.
- Advance Inked Indie to prototype visual-system exploration.
- Continue both for one more constrained test.
- Stop one or both because UI/world integration or production viability failed.

No review outcome should mark a final visual direction approved.
