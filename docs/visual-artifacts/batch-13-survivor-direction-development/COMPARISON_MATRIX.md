## What Would Change The Read

Soft Pixel advances over Inked Indie if:

- UI states feel native and crisp.
- The place card remains legible.
- Local storefront detail is stronger than generic pixel charm.
- The style tile shows a practical modular system.

Inked Indie advances over Soft Pixel if:

- Marker, hover, and place-card states remain precise over linework.
- The frame stays map-first rather than illustration-first.
- Local specificity remains visibly stronger.
- The style tile shows repeatable rules instead of one-off drawing taste.

## Borrowing Boundaries

Allowed:

- Soft Pixel may borrow Civic Atlas clarity and a small amount of Xerox sticker/flyer texture.
- Inked Indie may borrow Civic Atlas hierarchy and limited Xerox surface pressure.

Not allowed:

- Do not merge the two survivors into one hybrid.
- Do not revive the other four directions as full alternatives.
- Do not borrow magical/fantasy props or toy-town material language.
