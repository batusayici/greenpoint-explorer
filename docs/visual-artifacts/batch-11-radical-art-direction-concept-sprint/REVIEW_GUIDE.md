# Batch 11 Review Guide

Label: **direction-selection guide / not final approval**
Date: 2026-05-26

## What This Review Should Decide

This batch should produce a short-list decision:

- Which one or two directions deserve deeper development next?
- Which directions should be rejected for now?
- Are there any surface-language elements worth borrowing into a survivor direction?

This batch should not approve final art, final UI language, production assets, real-place representation, architecture, or app implementation.

## What To Judge Now

### Distinctiveness

Does the direction create a first-screenshot identity that is separable from the other five at thumbnail scale?

Strong signal: the rendering medium, materiality, lighting, camera attitude, and UI affordance idea all feel like one coherent world.

Weak signal: the frame could be described as a skin, filter, palette swap, or mood layer over a generic isometric street.

### Emotional Fit

Does the direction make the map feel worth browsing and clicking?

Strong signal: the block feels inviting, specific, and active without turning into a character game, fantasy scene, or generic cozy illustration.

Weak signal: the frame is visually impressive but feels too poster-like, too sterile, too cute, too gloomy, or too detached from browsing.

### Greenpoint Specificity

Does the image imply Greenpoint-native architecture and street life through facades, signs, sidewalk objects, storefront density, and local texture?

Strong signal: brick storefront rhythm, awnings, roll gates, bikes, stickered poles, community-board/flyer logic, corner geometry, and independent-shop surfaces carry the specificity.

Weak signal: Greenpoint is mainly present as a label painted into an otherwise generic city.

### Clickability / Map Readability

Can a user understand this as a browsable exterior map with storefront click targets?

Strong signal: entrances, facades, sidewalks, curbs, markers, and selected-place affordances are visible without reading the prompt.

Weak signal: texture, lighting, collage, depth of field, or illustration density overwhelms the clickable structure.

### Visual Taste Level

Does the direction feel controlled, authored, and culturally awake without leaning on generic startup polish, generic cozy game tropes, or empty coolness?

Strong signal: the image has taste pressure and local attitude, but still respects product clarity.

Weak signal: the frame feels like a genre default: retro pixel city, mobile toy town, municipal map, grunge poster, fantasy dusk scene, or generic indie comic panel.

### Production Survivability

Could this become a repeatable visual system for many storefronts, states, markers, cards, and zoom levels?

Strong signal: the image implies modular rules that can scale without losing identity.

Weak signal: the frame depends on one-off artistry, heavy texture, fragile lighting, or hand-authored details that would be hard to control.

### UI / World Compatibility

Can future UI overlays live inside this world without feeling pasted on?

Strong signal: the frame already suggests native marker logic, selection states, and place-card material.

Weak signal: the world is attractive but the marker/card either disappears, fights the image, or looks like a generic overlay.

## What Not To Over-Index On Yet

- Exact text inside generated signs. Treat generated lettering as mood evidence, not approved copy.
- Exact real-world placement, adjacency, addresses, or business truth.
- Final color palette.
- Final marker shape or card typography.
- Small image-generation artifacts.
- Whether every UI state is solved. Only hero frames exist now.
- Whether a single direction can already be productionized. The next batch exists to test that.

## Review Order

1. First pass: use `comparison-board.svg` at thumbnail scale and eliminate directions that do not separate visually or do not feel product-fit.
2. Second pass: inspect each full-size image path from `FRAME_INDEX.md`.
3. Third pass: compare the remaining candidates against clickability, UI/world compatibility, and production survivability.
4. Final pass: choose one or two survivor directions for the development batch.

## Expected Output From Batu

A useful decision can be as concrete as:

- Advance Soft Pixel Neighborhood Sim and Inked Indie Graphic Novel.
- Reject Tilt-Shift Toy Block and Civic Atlas for now.
- Borrow limited Xerox-Riso surface detail into one survivor.
- Do not treat any direction as final.

The decision should be visual and comparative, not prose-only approval.
