# Decision Memo

Label: **survivor recommendation / not final approval**
Date: 2026-05-26
Prepared by: Codex as junior creative / visual development assistant

## Recommendation

Advance two survivor directions into a focused development batch:

1. Soft Pixel Neighborhood Sim.
2. Inked Indie Graphic Novel.

Do not treat either as approved final direction. The next batch should prove whether they can support UI overlays, hover states, place cards, markers, map readability, and repeatable production rules.

## Why These Two Survive

### 1. Soft Pixel Neighborhood Sim

This is the strongest immediate MVP-fit direction.

The generated frame reads as a browsable exterior map at a glance: storefronts, entrances, sidewalk, street furniture, signage, and marker logic are all legible. It has warmth and clickability without requiring a heavy UI layer to explain what the product does. It also feels closest to a web-first clickable diorama because the world already behaves like a place the user can scan.

Why it advances:

- Best clickability and map readability.
- Strong storefront-led browsing feel.
- Good emotional fit: warm, playful, controlled, and inviting.
- UI markers can plausibly become native pixel-sticker or game-map affordances.
- Production risk is real but manageable if the style becomes HD pixel-inspired rather than strict handcrafted pixel art.

Main risk:

It can become generic retro-neighborhood charm or collapse back toward the older Community Pixel territory. The next batch must prove sharper Greenpoint specificity, stronger subculture detail, and a less generic marker/card system.

### 2. Inked Indie Graphic Novel

This is the strongest authored-taste and local-specificity direction.

The generated frame has the best storefront identity and street-level detail. It feels less generic than the atlas and toy directions, more controlled than the riso and magical realism directions, and more culturally specific than a clean isometric map. It gives the project a sharper editorial and creative-neighborhood voice while still preserving exterior browsing logic.

Why it advances:

- Strongest Greenpoint-specific facade and street texture.
- Best balance of visual taste, local grit, and controlled readability.
- Feels distinctive without needing fantasy or overbuilt UI clutter.
- Can support a place-browser if marker and card rules are developed with restraint.
- Offers a credible alternative to pixel charm: more authored, more adult, more local.

Main risk:

It may be hard to scale. The next batch must prove whether the inked style can become a repeatable visual system rather than a one-off illustration.

## Why The Other Four Do Not Advance Now

### Xerox-Riso Street Zine

This is one of the most distinctive frames, and its DIY material language is valuable. It should not advance as a full direction yet because the current frame leans too much toward poster/collage logic. The map remains visible, but the UI hierarchy and click targets are not durable enough.

Possible merge use:

Borrow surface texture, sticker logic, flyer density, and community-board energy into Inked Indie or Soft Pixel as controlled accents.

### Tilt-Shift Toy Block Greenpoint

This is polished and physically charming, but the image feels more like a beautiful miniature product than a Greenpoint-native cultural direction. It risks generic mobile-game toy town, and the simplification removes some of the edge the project needs.

Possible merge use:

Borrow physical marker thinking only if a survivor needs a more tangible selection-state system.

### Civic Atlas Deluxe

This is the safest and most production-friendly direction. It is also the least emotionally distinctive. The frame is elegant and readable, but it risks becoming a premium city-guide plate rather than a locally specific clickable diorama.

Possible merge use:

Borrow information hierarchy, restraint, and map clarity into both survivors.

### Weird Hyperlocal Magical Realism

This is memorable and atmospheric, but the generated frame overshoots the project guardrails. The moon, glowing props, waterfront surrealism, and dusk drama move toward fantasy/mood art. It is too risky as a primary direction for a map-mode MVP with no fantasy-game language.

Possible merge use:

Borrow controlled dusk lighting or warm window glow only if Batu wants a higher-emotion lighting pass later.

## Recommended Merge Opportunities

Do not merge directions yet as a new hybrid. That would collapse the divergence too early.

For the next batch, keep Soft Pixel and Inked Indie separate. Allow only limited accent borrowing:

- Soft Pixel may borrow Civic Atlas clarity and a small amount of Xerox sticker/flyer texture.
- Inked Indie may borrow Civic Atlas hierarchy and limited Xerox surface pressure.
- Neither survivor should borrow Weird Hyperlocal magical props until Batu explicitly asks for that emotional direction.

## Decision Boundary

This memo recommends survivor directions only.

Batu still owns:

- Which direction or directions advance.
- Whether to include any merge/accent instructions.
- Whether either survivor eventually becomes the final visual direction.
- Whether human visual design should enter before the next batch.
