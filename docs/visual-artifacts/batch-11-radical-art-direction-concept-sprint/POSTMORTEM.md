# Postmortem: Why The Previous Batch Failed

Label: **critique / corrective guidance / not final direction**  
Date: 2026-05-26

## Summary

The previous art-direction output did not meet the needs of a radical concept-selection gate. It produced style variants, not concept territories. The variants shared too much composition, scene logic, and artifact language, so Batu was asked to compare surface treatments rather than choose between meaningfully different visual worlds.

The failure was not that the artifacts were useless. They clarified some constraints around storefront-led browsing, UI markers, and local texture. The failure was that they were presented too close to a decision-making role that they could not actually support.

## Failure Points

### Insufficient Divergence

The options did not separate far enough in medium, materiality, camera attitude, world logic, UI/world relationship, or emotional promise.

Most differences lived in:

- Palette.
- Iconography.
- Label density.
- Texture density.
- Small marker and card treatments.

Those differences are useful later, after a territory is chosen. They are not enough for an early art-direction decision.

### Over-Reuse Of Base Composition

The shared base composition made the variants feel like skins of one drawing.

For controlled refinement, a shared composition can isolate one variable. For radical concept selection, it suppresses the real questions: what kind of world is this, what does the camera believe, how does the UI belong to the world, and what emotional promise does the first screenshot make?

### SVG Diagramming Bias

The previous artifacts leaned toward SVG diagramming and controlled symbolic construction. That helped truth labeling and layout discipline, but it also flattened materiality, touch, atmosphere, and authored visual language.

The result was too close to a diagram of an art direction and too far from visual evidence of one.

### Exterior / Interior Ambiguity

The project requires exterior map-mode browsing. Some prior language and visual implications drifted toward venue interiors, cutaways, social scenes, or mood vignettes.

The next pass must lock the view to exterior facades, sidewalks, street corners, signs, markers, and place-card logic.

### Prose Substituting For Visual Evidence

The previous package asked written descriptions to carry too much of the intended feeling. Phrases like "warm community storefront energy" or "DIY flyer influence" cannot stand in for actual visual evidence.

If Batu must imagine the storefront detail, UI integration, local specificity, or emotional tone after reading the prose, the artifact is not decision-grade.

### Codex Acting Like A Concept Artist Instead Of A Production / Governance Agent

Codex overstepped the useful role for this phase by trying to produce the visual answer inside a constrained diagramming format.

The better role is:

- Define divergent territories.
- Specify what visual evidence must be produced.
- Prepare prompts and review rubrics.
- Label constraints and risks.
- Keep approval authority with Batu.

Codex should not claim final taste authority or mark any direction approved.

## Corrective Principle

The next visual exploration must be a radical concept sprint, not another style-variant board.

Each candidate must differ in enough decision-relevant dimensions that it can survive a 20% zoom test. A reviewer should be able to tell, from thumbnails alone, that the options are different visual worlds.
