## Review Intent

Use the three revised SVG variants to decide whether the hybrid real-plus-placeholder composition is worth continuing, and which emotional volume should guide the next visual-preproduction pass.

The artifact passes only if Batu can choose or reject a direction from the visuals themselves.

## Questions For Batu

1. Does the slice feel worth continuing?
2. Does the hybrid real/placeholding approach feel acceptable?
3. Which emotional volume is strongest: quiet, balanced, or community-forward?
4. Are markers/cards helping or cluttering?
5. Does Peter Pan plus the G station provide enough identity?
6. Is storefront fictionality acceptable, or does it weaken trust?

## Variant-Specific Read

Quiet:
- Calm documentary storefront read with visible doors, windows, awnings, frames, and sidewalk context.
- Best test for whether the slice can stay sparse while still feeling local and specific.
- Risk: may still feel too reserved for the eventual interactive diorama.

Balanced:
- Likely target-direction test: warm, readable, distinctive, and clickable.
- Best test for whether marker/card hierarchy, storefront specificity, and modest community texture can coexist.
- Risk: may be the safest option rather than the strongest one.

Community-forward:
- Upper-bound density test with heavier DIY flyer/pixel/community energy.
- Best test for whether posters, stickers, signage, markers, and a louder card add life or become too overlay-led.
- Risk: may feel too game-like or too visually crowded.

## Rejection Checks

Reject the revision if:

- The variants still differ mainly by color.
- The storefronts still read as flat panels.
- Quiet feels empty rather than calm and specific.
- Balanced does not feel like a plausible target direction.
- Community-forward does not visibly test the density upper bound.
- Marker/card hierarchy is not legible from the visuals.
- The truth-status system is unclear.

## Do Not Approve From This Package

Do not approve:

- Final visual language.
- Production placement.
- Real-place cards.
- Exact storefront geometry.
- Exact station stair, elevator, or entrance placement.
- App implementation.

## Decision Needed Next

Batu should choose one of these next steps:

- Continue with one emotional-volume direction for a tighter follow-up artifact.
- Combine specific traits from two variants and request a revised comparison.
- Pause the compact Peter Pan plus G station slice because the hybrid strategy does not feel trustworthy or compelling enough.
