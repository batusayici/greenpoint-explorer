# Phase 6 Review + Prototype Translation Plan

Status: Docs-only review packet  
Date: 2026-05-28

## Purpose

This packet reviews the active Phase 6 repeatable assetization proof and translates its reusable visual-system findings into supporting evidence for prototype implementation.

`docs/CURRENT_EXECUTION_BRIEF.md` is the executable source of truth. The files in this packet, including `NEXT_CODEX_IMPLEMENTATION_BRIEF.md`, are retained as supporting review material only and are not a second active authority.

This packet does not execute implementation. It does not approve production assets, a production asset pipeline, broad Greenpoint coverage, real business data, exact addresses, factual place copy, live data, routing, CMS, persistence, deployment, or public interfaces.

## What Phase 6 Proved

Phase 6 provides credible review evidence that the approved Inked Indie / Compact Corner direction can support a small repeatable visual system:

- Three storefront archetypes can be visually distinguished: narrow service bay, two-bay retail pair, and compact corner/kiosk edge.
- Sign bands, awnings, windows, doors, prop clusters, posters, stickers, identity glyphs, markers, selected outlines, card anchors, and compact controls can be described as reusable families.
- The UI/card/marker language can stay visually connected to the raster world when it follows the approved paper/card integration references.
- A small 3-5 storefront street slice can carry repeated but varied facade logic without immediately collapsing into generic SVG-grade placeholder work.

## What Phase 6 Did Not Prove

Phase 6 did not prove production readiness.

It did not produce separated production assets, masks, anchor metadata, collision rules, hit regions, responsive behavior, accessibility behavior, real data cards, exact addresses, exact facades, exact station geometry, or a production asset pipeline.

The Phase 6 kit is proof-visible and documented. Most parts are not separately exported. The generated text and glyphs remain review-only and cannot become product copy.

## What Should Happen Next

The next implementation batch should be a constrained prototype integration pass:

- Use one approved Phase 6 raster as the primary review-only world plate.
- Preserve existing pan, zoom, hover, click, tap, and selected-card behavior.
- Add product-facing marker/card/tether/control styling inspired by the approved corpus and Phase 6 rules.
- Keep the scene fictional-safe and review-only.
- Avoid SVG/code-drawn storefronts or a new renderer.

The active next task is defined only in `docs/CURRENT_EXECUTION_BRIEF.md`.
