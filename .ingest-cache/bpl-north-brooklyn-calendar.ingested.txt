Greenpoint Bike Clinic Repair and Maintenance Pop-up
Sat, Sep 19 1:00 pm
Greenpoint, Demonstration Garden
Join us in the Demonstration Garden for free community-based bicycle repair and maintenance led by Greenpoint Bike Clinic.
Community members are invited to bring their bikes for:
basic tune-ups
flat repairs
brake adjustments
shifter adjustments
The clinic can also help riders identify parts they may need and offer guidance for anyone interested in learning more about maintaining their own bike.
All services are free, and open to all!
The goal is to provide practical support, empower neighbors to care for their bikes, and encourage healthy, low-carbon transportation in our community.
The Greenpoint Bike Clinic is a free, community-based bicycle repair and maintenance pop-up led by local resident Josh Rotbert, who has been involved in bicycle advocacy and repair workshops in Brooklyn for several years. Josh is passionate about making cycling more accessible, practical, and sustainable for everyday transportation, and started the clinic as a way to support neighbors who rely on their bikes to get around the city.

Sustainable Living Series: Tiny Treasures-Saving Seeds for Tomorrow
Sat, Oct 3 10:30 am
Greenpoint, Eco Lab 1
Part 2 of our 3-part Sustainable Living Series! Saving seeds is an easy way to support biodiversity, strengthen local food systems, and become a more self-reliant gardener. Join us for an introduction to seed saving and learn how to collect and preserve seeds for future harvests and community sharing. In honor of City of Forest Day, we'll also spend some time talking about collecting tree seeds to grow saplings!
Brooklyn Library presenter gil lopez is an environmental educator, community organizer and artists focused on sustainable urban development, equitable land use, waste reduction, and community-led environmental initiatives throughout New York City.

Sustainable Living Series: Branching Out-Street Tree Care Basics
Sat, Nov 14 10:30 am
Greenpoint, Eco Lab 1
Part 3 of our 3-part Sustainable Living Series! Take a deep dive into the ecological services that our urban trees provide for all of us and how to give them care in return. From increasing the health of the living soil food-web tree rely on, removing litter and loosening the soil so it can absorb more water. These lessons can be taken home and applied to any street tree in NYC, including in front your home, school, library, workplace and beyond.
Brooklyn Library presenter gil lopez is an environmental educator, community organizer and artists focused on sustainable urban development, equitable land use, waste reduction, and community-led environmental initiatives throughout New York City.
