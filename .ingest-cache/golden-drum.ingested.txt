Alash Ensemble, Kevin Nathaniel & Dream Seed Live in NYC
Wednesday, October 21, 2026
7:00 PM
9:30 PM
Golden Drum
97 Green St. G1
Brooklyn, NY, 11222
Unites States of America
(map)
Google Calendar
ICS
Join us at Golden Drum for a special evening of live music bringing together Alash Ensemble, Kevin Nathaniel, and Dream Seed for an exploration of rhythm, resonance, voice, and music from across cultures.
Alash Ensemble is a trio of internationally recognized master throat singers and instrumentalists from Tuva, a republic in the heart of Central Asia. They are renowned for Tuvan throat singing (khöömei) , an extraordinary vocal tradition in which a singer brings out multiple tones and harmonics within a single voice. Rooted in the musical traditions of Central Asian nomadic culture, Alash honors the music of their homeland while expanding it through influences from jazz, bluegrass, Eastern European folk music, and other contemporary styles.
Kevin Nathaniel and Dream Seed come together for a collaborative performance rooted in rhythm, improvisation, and the transformative possibilities of sound. Kevin Nathaniel is a master percussionist and mbira player whose music draws upon African and diasporic traditions, while Dream Seed creates immersive sound journeys using instruments and musical traditions from around the world. Together, they weave percussion, voice, melody, and meditative sound into a spontaneous musical experience that invites both deep listening and connection.
This concert offers a rare opportunity to experience these artists together in the intimate setting of Golden Drum, moving from the remarkable overtones and ancient vocal traditions of Tuva to the rhythmic and expansive soundscapes of Kevin Nathaniel and Dream Seed.
Come experience an evening of musicianship, cross-cultural musical traditions, improvisation, and the power of sound to bring people together.

About Alash Ensemble
Alash Ensemble are internationally acclaimed masters of Tuvan throat singing (xöömei), a remarkable vocal technique that allows a singer to produce multiple pitches at the same time. Trained in the traditional music of Tuva from childhood, the trio combines extraordinary vocal techniques with indigenous instruments such as the igil, doshpuluur, xomus, and kengirge. While deeply rooted in Tuvan musical heritage, Alash subtly incorporates contemporary influences, including complex harmonies, Western instruments, and modern song forms, creating music that is both innovative and unmistakably Tuvan.
Alash has toured extensively throughout the United States and internationally, earning acclaim from audiences and critics alike; The Washington Post called their music “utterly stunning.” Known for adventurous collaborations, they have performed with artists ranging from the legendary Sun Ra Arkestra to Béla Fleck & the Flecktones, appearing on the Grammy-winning album Jingle All the Way . In addition to performing, Alash is dedicated to teaching and cultural exchange, offering workshops at schools, universities, conservatories, and communities around the world and introducing new generations to the extraordinary musical traditions of Tuva.
https://www.alashensemble.com/
About Kevin Nathaniel
Kevin Nathaniel was born and raised in Detroit, Michigan—Motown and one of America’s great musical centers. Surrounded by extraordinary musicians and music from an early age, his own path as a musician began near the end of college, when encounters with music from Africa inspired him to begin playing, studying, and building instruments.
Kevin’s journey began with the mbira, also known as the kalimba, particularly the mbira traditions of Southern Africa and Zimbabwe. Learning to build and play these instruments opened the door to a lifelong exploration of music and an ever-expanding range of instruments and traditions. The mbira remains at the heart of his musical path, serving as the foundation for his work as a multi-instrumentalist, instrument maker, performer, and musical explorer.
https://www.instagram.com/kevinnathanielhealing
About Dream Seed
Dream Seed is a sound healing collective created by members of Golden Drum, Sacred Arts Research Foundation, and Didge Project. As students of Maestro Manuel Rufino, the members of Dream Seed are dedicated to the study and preservation of the sacred traditions of humanity and the continued exploration of sound as a healing modality of the Aquarian Age. Dream Seed studies and works with indigenous and traditional elders, continuously seeking to expand their knowledge of sound and healing as universal methods of cultivating harmony, peace, and well-being.
Members of Dream Seed include Brooke Gillespie, AJ Block, Jerry Walsh, Hannah Apollonia, Naku Kiwanuka, Matt Canale, Xango Shola, Mateusz Bazgier, Nicole Casanova, El Stone, Michael Fisher & Ixchel Prisma.
More info on Dream Seed at http://dreamseedsound.com/

ARA Healing Arts School Year Long Apprenticeship & Launch
Tuesday, November 3, 2026
7:00 PM
Tuesday, October 26, 2027
8:00 PM
Golden Drum
97 Green St. G1
Brooklyn, NY, 11222
Unites States of America
(map)

ICS
Golden Drum is pleased to announce the launch of ARA Healing Arts School , guided by community members Brooke Hamre Gillespie and Nicole Adriana Casanova . ARA Healing Arts honors and integrates the sacred traditions of humanity that support awakening, consciousness, and holistic healing. Brooke and Nicole bring over twenty years each of dedicated study and practice in traditional healing arts and spiritual lineages.
This year-long, 150-hour training program, beginning in September 2026, supports personal healing and awakening, while also addressing interpersonal and planetary healing across all levels of relationship—physical, emotional, mental, and spiritual. The course is designed to cultivate awareness, balance, and responsibility in how we relate to ourselves, others, and the world.
Sign up for an enrollment interview here
ARA Healing Arts School Apprenticeship is open to participants of all experience levels. Throughout the year, students will be introduced to a wide range of healing modalities and practices, including breath, dance, sound, sacred art, lightwork, astrology, yoga, Reiki, tarot, earth-based traditions, elemental work, Ayurveda, and other complementary healing techniques. Our training also illuminates friendship and good relationships as crucial for ongoing growth and well-being in our lives.
This extended format allows for a deep and holistic exploration of the healing arts, providing sustained support for each participant’s inner journey. Unlike short-term or weekend-based training, this year-long container offers the time, continuity, and personal guidance necessary to develop a grounded, foundational, and integrated healing practice. The program is intentionally designed to support long-term growth, self-awareness, and embodiment, fostering expanded consciousness, heightened sensitivity, and open-hearted presence.
The first year of ARA Healing Arts School focuses on self-discovery as the foundation of healing work. Participants will explore energetic anatomy, including the chakras and subtle bodies, as well as sound, frequency, astrology, embodiment practices, energetic hygiene, and the unique modalities that resonate with each individual’s healing path. As the year progresses, participants will also begin learning how to work with others, with an emphasis on ethical practice, self-awareness, and continual personal healing. Guest teachers will be invited throughout the year to share their specialized knowledge and lived experience with participants.
In addition, students will have the opportunity to participate in two immersive retreats: one held in Upstate New York and a second longer retreat offered at a location to be determined. These retreats provide an opportunity to step away from the rhythms of daily life and enter a dedicated learning environment where participants can deepen their practice, strengthen community connections, engage in ceremony, and experience extended periods of hands-on training, reflection, and personal transformation in a supportive and nature-based setting. Retreat information will be given upon registration in the course.
Through a combination of online and in-person workshops, activations, and experiential learning, participants are guided through a heart-centered journey designed to bring the mind, body, heart, and spirit into deeper alignment—with the self, the Earth, the cosmos, and all relations.
To join this life-affirming apprenticeship program FILL OUT THIS FORM and Brooke and Nicole will set up an interview with you.

Anahata Sessions: SEPTEMBER
Friday, September 11, 2026
7:00 PM
9:30 PM
Golden Drum
97 Green St. G1
Brooklyn, NY, 11222
Unites States of America
(map)
Google Calendar
ICS
Step into the rhythm of Virgo Season with an evening devoted to the hearth, celebrating and attuning to the symbol of the cornucopia and deeply connecting to the beauty of presence. This Anahata Session at the Golden Drum invites you to unearth the intelligence of the heart and mind in harmony in an embodied way.
Together, we will explore the current astrological transits through the lens of Virgo, an archetype of devotion, discernment, and sacred service. Through guided reflection, we contemplate how this season calls us to tend to the subtle details of our lives and the lives of those around us with love, intention, and integrity.
The evening will open in the field between sound and silence, allowing the mind to quiet and the heart center (Anahata) to gently awaken. From this point of clarity and stillness, you will be invited into a ritual of self-devotion: writing a love letter to yourself. This is an offering of tenderness, truth, and future blessing—words from your present self to the one you are becoming. These letters will be placed into a communal basket and later redistributed at random, allowing each participant to receive a mysterious message of love and insight—an echo from the collective heart, arriving right on time.
From this place, we will learn a sacred song, weaving our voices together in a devotional practice that harmonizes breath, body, and spirit. No prior singing experience is needed—only a willingness to listen and be heard. This is an evening of refinement and release, of sacred order and intuitive flow. A space to remember that devotion to self is devotion to all. Come as you are. Leave with a fuller heart.
In each Anahata Session, participants are guided through a unique, heart-centered journey that may include practices such as tarot, sound healing, lightwork, reiki, astrology, and ayurveda. Each month’s offerings are carefully selected to inspire insight, facilitate transformation, and bring the mind, body, and spirit into deeper alignment with the self, the earth, and the cosmos.
We are limited in space, so please buy your ticket ahead of time. Suggested donation $30 in advance, $40 at the door (no one will be turned away for lack of funds).
***This is a space for healing, clarity, and inner alignment. We kindly ask that you refrain from using any mind-altering substances prior to or during the evening, so you can be fully present and receive the evening’s offerings with openness and intention.

An evening of heart-centered, visionary folk music in service of personal and collective healing, liberation, and remembrance.
Songs for Healing & Liberation with Andy Fischer-Price & Rebecca Elatiki
$20.00
Purchase
Andy Fischer-Price is a singer/songwriter living in Chimacum, WA who sings songs in recognition and celebration of our inherent oneness. His music is a prayer for the healing and evolution of humanity, the breaking of intergenerational cycles of trauma, and the emergence of a world rooted in peace and freedom for all. Known for his truly dynamic and memorable live performances, Andy’s visionary folk and medicine songs serve as invitations into the heart space and bridges between the mystical and material realms. He hopes his music may play a part in the nurturing of a kinder, more conscious, and loving world.
His debut album Lujah came out on Bandcamp in May of 2023 and was universally released in October of 2024. An album of Lujah remixes was released in May of 2025 on Mose’s record label, Resueño. www.afpmusic.com
Andy’s Instagram
Rebecca Elatiki is a Moroccan-American singer and songwriter whose genre-bending sound reflects the richness of a mixed and multi-layered identity rooted in creative freedom. The daughter of a professional opera singer and an immigrant father, she draws from both her North African roots and Western upbringing to craft a sound that’s uniquely her own. With haunting, ethereal vocals and nods to Arab musical traditions, Rebecca seamlessly weaves ancient melodies into modern, experimental compositions—creating a sonic world that feels both familiar and otherworldly. www.rebeccaelatiki.com

African Drum Class with Abdoul Doumbia, Mali
Wednesday, December 9, 2026
7:00 PM
9:00 PM
Golden Drum
97 Green St. G1
Brooklyn, NY, 11222
Unites States of America
(map)
Google Calendar
ICS
“Djembe” means coming together and going together in peace. It’s an instrument that brings together our hearts when we all play together.
We are grateful to have traditional Djembe player, Abdoul Doumbia of Mali coming to teach us about the traditional healing rhythms and history of the Djembe. These classes are for all levels of players, there is an abundance of knowledge being shared and regardless of your experience, this is a special and rare opportunity to learn from a master.
*We will have some drums for use, but if you have a djembe we recommend bringing it. Thank you!
*No one turned away for lack of funds, please contact goldendruminquiries@gmail.com for assistance
12/09/26 Djembe Class
$30.00
Register Now
Abdoul Doumbia was born in Bamako, Mali in 1965 and was raised in the village of Foutaka Zambougou near the ancient Bamana city of Segou. At the age of five Abdoul began to learn the music of his culture. He went on to complete a 16 year apprenticeship with master drummer Moriba Keita in Bamako. Abdoul worked with many professional drum and dance companies in Mali, including nine years as lead drummer for the renowned traditional drum and dance troupe Babemba, and was chosen to represent his region for eight consecutive years in the National Drum Festival of Mali. Abdoul immigrated to the United States in 1991 to accept a position at Brown University. He taught regularly on the east coast, including classes at Yale, Harvard, Princeton, RISDI, the University of Florida, and the University of Connecticut before moving to Boulder, Colorado in 1998 where he currently lives and teaches. In Colorado Abdoul has taught classes at CU, Naropa and The Colorado College. Abdoul is known for his mastery of Malian djembe and dunun and is a respected source of information regarding Mali's rich culture and history.
https://abdouldoumbia.com

Dream Seed Sound Healer Training Program, 2026-2027
Saturday, October 10, 2026
10:00 AM
11:00 AM
Golden Drum
97 Green St. G1
Brooklyn, NY, 11222
Unites States of America
(map)
Google Calendar
ICS
A 120-Hour Training, In-Person and Online via Zoom, October 2026 through April 2027
The Dream Seed Sound Healer Training Program is an immersive multicultural course designed to give people a hands-on approach for facilitating sound healing sessions. Participants will strengthen their capacity as sound workers, walking away with the skills and knowledge to be able to begin to incorporate sound into healing sessions and meditative environments through an interdisciplinary approach, bridging the fields of sound, science, sacred music, energy work, and ceremonial healing traditions. This program is for people of all levels of experience with sound, music and healing work, and is great for those who want to go deeper into doing sound healing work for others as well as those who want to explore sound for personal practice. Participants who meet the attendance requirements will receive a certification of course completion.
Members of Dream Seed pictured from left to right: Naku Kiwanuka, AJ Block, Ixchel Prisma, Hannah Apollonia, Brooke Gillespie, Jerry Walsh, and Xango Shola. Not pictured: Matt Canale, Mateusz Bazgier, Nicole Casanova, Michael Fisher
Free Info Session about The Dream Seed Sound Healer Training 2026-2027, date tba: register here for updates.
Watch our Informational Video featuring Dream Seed facilitators and program alumni:
Throughout the course of this training, we will focus on various exercises to learn more about sound healing through intention setting, listening, giving, receiving, and developing our intuition in relationship to sound. Participants are encouraged but not required to bring their own sound healing instruments to our sessions. Many instruments from around the world will be available for participants to utilize and a variety of sound-healing instruments will also be available for sale.
The training course will include 2 weekend intensives in upstate New York (see schedule) where participants will go deep into traditional healing practices from around the globe. The upstate retreats will be open to both in-person and remote participants but will require an additional fee by remote participants to attend.
*Note for online-only remote participants: course materials will consist of a combination of live Zoom meetings and pre-recorded content from our program. Video recordings will be made available immediately after each live session in case you are not able to attend, which will remain available through September 1, 2027. Participants will have the option to download the zoom meetings and video recordings to keep indefinitely. Online-only registrations do not include the 2-day retreats in the Catskills, but participants are welcome to attend for an additional cost.

-Shruti box and harmonium

-Other ethnic instruments from around the world
Creating a Sacred Container

Personal Well-Being Practices and Studies

*Notes:
The retreats in the Catskills will be Saturday through Sunday, with the option to arrive at the accommodations on Friday night. Dormitory sleeping in the main event space is included at no cost and cabins are available for $100/weekend shared and $200/weekend private.
Workshops at Golden Drum (denoted “at GD”) are 6 hours with a 1-hour break on Saturdays, usually 1-7pm.
Additionally this in-person training includes:
5x Exclusive group zoom meetings (90-minutes each, scheduled on select Wednesday evenings, 7:30-9pm EST)
2x 1-on-1 phone check-ins with the program facilitators
Homework assignments that will get you doing sound work on your own and with other people.
A number of pre-recorded video lectures and workshops.

What is sound healing and what is it used for?
Sound is our essence. We are vibration. Sound is one of the prominent medicines of the Aquarian Age. The world is waking up to “new age” therapies rooted in sound and vibration that are in fact ancient. Sound rituals have been an integral part of indigenous and spiritual traditions and cultures worldwide for thousands of years. In every indigenous culture, it is deeply understood that sound is an essential element of creation, celebration, and well-being. Today, science is catching up to this understanding and sound is growing as a mainstream therapeutic practice for consciousness, healing, and wellbeing.
Classes will be recorded. There will be different classes for in-person and online remote participants. Both in-person & online students will receive near-immediate access to the class recordings and will be able to access them for up to 3 months after the end of the program. Students can download the content on their own to keep as long as they want.

About Dream Seed
Dream Seed is a sound healing collective created by members of Golden Drum, Sacred Arts Research Foundation, and Didge Project. As students of Maestro Manuel Rufino, the members of Dream Seed are dedicated to the study and preservation of the sacred traditions of humanity and the continued exploration of sound as a healing modality of the Aquarian Age. Dream Seed studies and works with indigenous and traditional elders, continuously seeking to expand their knowledge of sound and healing as universal methods of cultivating harmony, peace, and well-being. More info on Dream Seed at http://dreamseedsound.com/
Members of Dream Seed conducting the Sound Healer Training include Brooke Gillespie, AJ Block, Jerry Walsh, Hannah Apollonia, Naku Kiwanuka, Matt Canale, Xango Shola, Mateusz Bazgier, Michael Fisher & Ixchel Prisma.

Unveiling the Temple Body with the Sacred Roses by Ananda (Brazil)
Tuesday, August 25, 2026
7:00 PM
9:30 PM
Golden Drum
97 Green St. G1
Brooklyn, NY, 11222
Unites States of America
(map)
Google Calendar
ICS
Join Ananda for a two-hour experiential workshop exploring the body as a space for awareness, transformation, and deeper connection with ourselves.
Drawing from her many years of spiritual study and body-centered practices, Ananda will introduce the concept of the Temple Body —an invitation to experience the body not simply as something we inhabit, but as a source of wisdom, presence, and inner guidance.
The workshop will begin with a brief introduction to Ananda’s journey and the foundations of her work, followed by a guided body-awareness practice inspired by Core Energetics Body Therapy . Through conscious movement, breath, sound, tension and release, participants will be invited to notice where the body holds patterns of stress, protection, and emotional tension, while creating more space for presence, vitality, and authentic expression.
From this more grounded and embodied state, Ananda will guide participants into the Sacred Roses practice , a symbolic and energetic activation connected to the Pentagram of Venus. The roses represent qualities of beauty, harmony, love, creativity, and the integration of our receptive and active energies.
Rather than approaching these ideas only intellectually, the workshop invites participants to experience them through the body—to listen inwardly, reconnect with their own inner wisdom, and explore what may be ready to soften, shift, or emerge.
The experience will weave together movement, conscious breathing, sound, embodied awareness, guided energetic practice, and moments of integration .
No previous experience is necessary. The workshop is open to everyone who feels curious to explore a deeper relationship with the body, awareness, and their own inner world.

7:00 PM
9:30 PM

97 Green St. G1
Brooklyn, NY, 11222
Unites States of America
(map)
Google Calendar
ICS
Sagittarius season invites us to expand our perspective. Ruled by Jupiter, Sagittarius is associated with exploration, truth-seeking, higher learning, philosophy, faith, and the search for meaning. It asks us to look beyond the immediate details of our lives and consider the larger story: What are we learning, and what does it mean?
The Sagittarius–Gemini axis speaks to the movement from knowledge to wisdom . Gemini gathers information, asks questions, notices details, and remains curious about different perspectives. Sagittarius takes those experiences and begins to synthesize them—to find patterns, meaning, and a larger understanding. Knowledge is what we collect; wisdom emerges when we integrate what we've learned and allow it to shape how we see and live.
Under the Gemini Full Moon, this process can become especially visible. A conversation, piece of information, or realization may suddenly connect dots that have been accumulating for some time. We may find ourselves questioning the stories we've been telling, noticing where our beliefs have outgrown the information that formed them, or recognizing where we've been gathering more knowledge without taking the time to integrate it.
This Full Moon invites us to hold both ends of the axis: stay curious without becoming scattered, and seek meaning without becoming overly certain. Rather than asking only, “What do I know?” , we might ask: “What have I learned, what has it changed in me, and what wisdom am I ready to carry forward?”
Join us for this moment of conscious connection through a discussion about the astrology, sound bath, integration circle with tea and more.

Thursday, August 13, 2026

9:00 PM
Golden Drum
97 Green St. G1

Unites States of America
(map)

ICS
“Djembe” means coming together and going together in peace. It’s an instrument that brings together our hearts when we all play together.
We are grateful to have traditional Djembe player, Abdoul Doumbia of Mali coming to teach us about the traditional rhythms and history of the Djembe. These classes are for all levels of players, there is an abundance of knowledge being shared and regardless of your experience, this is a special and rare opportunity to learn from a master.
*We will have some drums for use, but if you have a djembe we recommend bringing it. Thank you!
*No one turned away for lack of funds, please contact goldendruminquiries@gmail.com for assistance
African Drum Circle 06/03/26
$30.00
Purchase
Abdoul Doumbia was born in Bamako, Mali in 1965 and was raised in the village of Foutaka Zambougou near the ancient Bamana city of Segou. At the age of five Abdoul began to learn the music of his culture. He went on to complete a 16 year apprenticeship with master drummer Moriba Keita in Bamako. Abdoul worked with many professional drum and dance companies in Mali, including nine years as lead drummer for the renowned traditional drum and dance troupe Babemba, and was chosen to represent his region for eight consecutive years in the National Drum Festival of Mali. Abdoul immigrated to the United States in 1991 to accept a position at Brown University. He taught regularly on the east coast, including classes at Yale, Harvard, Princeton, RISDI, the University of Florida, and the University of Connecticut before moving to Boulder, Colorado in 1998 where he currently lives and teaches. In Colorado Abdoul has taught classes at CU, Naropa and The Colorado College. Abdoul is known for his mastery of Malian djembe and dunun and is a respected source of information regarding Mali's rich culture and history.

Anahata Sessions: AUGUST
Tuesday, August 11, 2026
7:00 PM
9:30 PM
Golden Drum
97 Green St. G1
Brooklyn, NY, 11222
Unites States of America
(map)
Google Calendar
ICS
"Yesterday I was clever, so I wanted to change the world. Today I am wise, so I am changing myself." - RUMI
In the height of mid-summer, we gather in celebration of the radiant energy of creation.
As the Sun moves through Leo, we are invited into the heart—into courage, expression, and the living light that animates all things. The Sun, center of our solar system and source of life, reminds us of our own inner luminosity and our connection to the greater web of existence. At the same time, we are in the aura of the full moon lunar eclipse in Aquarius, which shines a light on all of the ways we are working to express our individual vision within the context of the greater collective.
This eclipse signals a new two year nodal-cycle, a collective story unfolding within the context of the lunar nodes shifting to help us align our heart (Leo) with our mind (Aquarius). Known to some as the axis of Ego and to others as the vertical axis of the Spirit, we are gifted the opportunity to transcend our limitiations and transform our relationship with ourselves.
This an opportunity to slow down, reconnect, and enter into a deeper relationship with the light within and around you.
The evening will include teachings and study of the Sun in Leo and Moon in Aquarius, a heart-opening guided meditation, heart-song freeing the voice session, and a flower-blessing ceremony. We will close with time for reflection, integration, and connection within a supportive container.
Each participant will receive a sunflower for continued meditation, and a pillar candle to carry the practice forward beyond our time together.
This Anahata Session is both a celebration and an initiation, an invitation to align with the rhythm of the Sun, soften into the wisdom of the heart, and remember your place within the greater field of creation.
All are welcome to join in this shared experience of light, beauty, and connection.

In this Leo season we are invited to go inside and connect with our inner fire. Where do we shine? Where do we want to shine, but are holding ourselves back? This is a time of creativity and allowing for self-expression, cultivating the willpower to dare to be our own unique self. So join us for a sound bath, astrological talk, warm tea and more to connect with your individuality.
Join us for this moment of self-care through conscious connection to the astrological moment, a sound bath, warm tea and more.
Bring a pen, paper, and if you feel called, something to charge under this new moon energy.

Cassie Luzenski is a multi-disciplinary artist, creating experiences through her training as a designer (B.A, in Creative Direction, Design), Sound Meditation Facilitator, Yoga teacher and Shamanic Reiki practitioner. With a deep passion for holistic well-being, spiritual studies and designing intentional spaces, Cassie has spent the past three years immersing herself in a variety of spiritual, cultural, and traditional practices with many elders and wisdom-keepers around the world. She is working to merge the world of art, music and healing.
Adrian DiMatteo (B.M. Eastman School of Music, 2012) is the founder of the Sonic Institute. He is an international performing and recording artist, author ( Elements of Sound: A Full-Spectrum Exploration of Sound and Consciousness ), music educator, app creator ( Chord Atlas ), and sound meditation facilitator with over 20 years’ experience in the music world. Adrian has toured and traveled extensively—exploring sound, language, musical culture and how they affect individual and collective wellbeing. Hundreds of students have graduated from Sound Healer Training programs and weekend retreats that Adrian regularly facilitates nationwide. To date, he has offered musical experiences to thousands of people in hospitals, hospices, elder care facilities, prisons and children’s centers.
The Sonic Institute offers experiential workshops, music lessons, healing sessions, events and training designed to help people connect with sound and music as a tool for meditation and healing. Our programs unite ancient spirituality with modern science to reveal the impact of sound vibration on health and consciousness. Through online & in-person lessons, sound baths, presentations and interactive workshops, participants gain skills and knowledge needed to facilitate guided meditation and sound healing experiences.
