ARA Healing Arts School Year Long Apprenticeship & Launch

Tuesday, October 26, 2027

Golden Drum is pleased to announce the launch of ARA Healing Arts School , guided by community members Brooke Hamre Gillespie and Nicole Adriana Casanova . ARA Healing Arts honors and integrates the sacred traditions of humanity that support awakening, consciousness, and holistic healing. Brooke and Nicole bring over twenty years each of dedicated study and practice in traditional healing arts and spiritual lineages.
This year-long, 150-hour training program, beginning in September 2026, supports personal healing and awakening, while also addressing interpersonal and planetary healing across all levels of relationship—physical, emotional, mental, and spiritual. The course is designed to cultivate awareness, balance, and responsibility in how we relate to ourselves, others, and the world.
Sign up for an enrollment interview here
ARA Healing Arts School Apprenticeship is open to participants of all experience levels. Throughout the year, students will be introduced to a wide range of healing modalities and practices, including breath, dance, sound, sacred art, lightwork, astrology, yoga, Reiki, tarot, earth-based traditions, elemental work, Ayurveda, and other complementary healing techniques. Our training also illuminates friendship and good relationships as crucial for ongoing growth and well-being in our lives.
This extended format allows for a deep and holistic exploration of the healing arts, providing sustained support for each participant’s inner journey. Unlike short-term or weekend-based training, this year-long container offers the time, continuity, and personal guidance necessary to develop a grounded, foundational, and integrated healing practice. The program is intentionally designed to support long-term growth, self-awareness, and embodiment, fostering expanded consciousness, heightened sensitivity, and open-hearted presence.
The first year of ARA Healing Arts School focuses on self-discovery as the foundation of healing work. Participants will explore energetic anatomy, including the chakras and subtle bodies, as well as sound, frequency, astrology, embodiment practices, energetic hygiene, and the unique modalities that resonate with each individual’s healing path. As the year progresses, participants will also begin learning how to work with others, with an emphasis on ethical practice, self-awareness, and continual personal healing. Guest teachers will be invited throughout the year to share their specialized knowledge and lived experience with participants.
In addition, students will have the opportunity to participate in two immersive retreats: one held in Upstate New York and a second longer retreat offered at a location to be determined. These retreats provide an opportunity to step away from the rhythms of daily life and enter a dedicated learning environment where participants can deepen their practice, strengthen community connections, engage in ceremony, and experience extended periods of hands-on training, reflection, and personal transformation in a supportive and nature-based setting. Retreat information will be given upon registration in the course.
Through a combination of online and in-person workshops, activations, and experiential learning, participants are guided through a heart-centered journey designed to bring the mind, body, heart, and spirit into deeper alignment—with the self, the Earth, the cosmos, and all relations.
To join this life-affirming apprenticeship program FILL OUT THIS FORM and Brooke and Nicole will set up an interview with you.

A 120-Hour Training, In-Person and Online via Zoom, October 2026 through April 2027
The Dream Seed Sound Healer Training Program is an immersive multicultural course designed to give people a hands-on approach for facilitating sound healing sessions. Participants will strengthen their capacity as sound workers, walking away with the skills and knowledge to be able to begin to incorporate sound into healing sessions and meditative environments through an interdisciplinary approach, bridging the fields of sound, science, sacred music, energy work, and ceremonial healing traditions. This program is for people of all levels of experience with sound, music and healing work, and is great for those who want to go deeper into doing sound healing work for others as well as those who want to explore sound for personal practice. Participants who meet the attendance requirements will receive a certification of course completion.

Throughout the course of this training, we will focus on various exercises to learn more about sound healing through intention setting, listening, giving, receiving, and developing our intuition in relationship to sound. Participants are encouraged but not required to bring their own sound healing instruments to our sessions. Many instruments from around the world will be available for participants to utilize and a variety of sound-healing instruments will also be available for sale.
The training course will include 2 weekend intensives in upstate New York (see schedule) where participants will go deep into traditional healing practices from around the globe. The upstate retreats will be open to both in-person and remote participants but will require an additional fee by remote participants to attend.
*Note for online-only remote participants: course materials will consist of a combination of live Zoom meetings and pre-recorded content from our program. Video recordings will be made available immediately after each live session in case you are not able to attend, which will remain available through September 1, 2027. Participants will have the option to download the zoom meetings and video recordings to keep indefinitely. Online-only registrations do not include the 2-day retreats in the Catskills, but participants are welcome to attend for an additional cost.

What is sound healing and what is it used for?
Sound is our essence. We are vibration. Sound is one of the prominent medicines of the Aquarian Age. The world is waking up to “new age” therapies rooted in sound and vibration that are in fact ancient. Sound rituals have been an integral part of indigenous and spiritual traditions and cultures worldwide for thousands of years. In every indigenous culture, it is deeply understood that sound is an essential element of creation, celebration, and well-being. Today, science is catching up to this understanding and sound is growing as a mainstream therapeutic practice for consciousness, healing, and wellbeing.

About Dream Seed
Dream Seed is a sound healing collective created by members of Golden Drum, Sacred Arts Research Foundation, and Didge Project. As students of Maestro Manuel Rufino, the members of Dream Seed are dedicated to the study and preservation of the sacred traditions of humanity and the continued exploration of sound as a healing modality of the Aquarian Age. Dream Seed studies and works with indigenous and traditional elders, continuously seeking to expand their knowledge of sound and healing as universal methods of cultivating harmony, peace, and well-being. More info on Dream Seed at http://dreamseedsound.com/

African Drum Circle with Abdoul Doumbia, Mali presented by Sounds of Liberation
Thursday, August 13, 2026
7:00 PM
9:00 PM
Golden Drum
97 Green St. G1
Brooklyn, NY, 11222
Unites States of America
(map)

“Djembe” means coming together and going together in peace. It’s an instrument that brings together our hearts when we all play together.
We are grateful to have traditional Djembe player, Abdoul Doumbia of Mali coming to teach us about the traditional rhythms and history of the Djembe. These classes are for all levels of players, there is an abundance of knowledge being shared and regardless of your experience, this is a special and rare opportunity to learn from a master.
*We will have some drums for use, but if you have a djembe we recommend bringing it. Thank you!
*No one turned away for lack of funds, please contact goldendruminquiries@gmail.com for assistance
African Drum Circle 06/03/26
$30.00
Purchase
Abdoul Doumbia was born in Bamako, Mali in 1965 and was raised in the village of Foutaka Zambougou near the ancient Bamana city of Segou. At the age of five Abdoul began to learn the music of his culture. He went on to complete a 16 year apprenticeship with master drummer Moriba Keita in Bamako. Abdoul worked with many professional drum and dance companies in Mali, including nine years as lead drummer for the renowned traditional drum and dance troupe Babemba, and was chosen to represent his region for eight consecutive years in the National Drum Festival of Mali. Abdoul immigrated to the United States in 1991 to accept a position at Brown University. He taught regularly on the east coast, including classes at Yale, Harvard, Princeton, RISDI, the University of Florida, and the University of Connecticut before moving to Boulder, Colorado in 1998 where he currently lives and teaches. In Colorado Abdoul has taught classes at CU, Naropa and The Colorado College. Abdoul is known for his mastery of Malian djembe and dunun and is a respected source of information regarding Mali's rich culture and history.

Anahata Sessions: AUGUST
Tuesday, August 11, 2026
7:00 PM
9:30 PM
Golden Drum
97 Green St. G1
Brooklyn, NY, 11222
Unites States of America
(map)
Google Calendar

"Yesterday I was clever, so I wanted to change the world. Today I am wise, so I am changing myself." - RUMI
In the height of mid-summer, we gather in celebration of the radiant energy of creation.
As the Sun moves through Leo, we are invited into the heart—into courage, expression, and the living light that animates all things. The Sun, center of our solar system and source of life, reminds us of our own inner luminosity and our connection to the greater web of existence. At the same time, we are in the aura of the full moon lunar eclipse in Aquarius, which shines a light on all of the ways we are working to express our individual vision within the context of the greater collective.
This eclipse signals a new two year nodal-cycle, a collective story unfolding within the context of the lunar nodes shifting to help us align our heart (Leo) with our mind (Aquarius). Known to some as the axis of Ego and to others as the vertical axis of the Spirit, we are gifted the opportunity to transcend our limitiations and transform our relationship with ourselves.
This an opportunity to slow down, reconnect, and enter into a deeper relationship with the light within and around you.
The evening will include teachings and study of the Sun in Leo and Moon in Aquarius, a heart-opening guided meditation, heart-song freeing the voice session, and a flower-blessing ceremony. We will close with time for reflection, integration, and connection within a supportive container.
Each participant will receive a sunflower for continued meditation, and a pillar candle to carry the practice forward beyond our time together.
This Anahata Session is both a celebration and an initiation, an invitation to align with the rhythm of the Sun, soften into the wisdom of the heart, and remember your place within the greater field of creation.
All are welcome to join in this shared experience of light, beauty, and connection.

Cassie Luzenski is a multi-disciplinary artist, creating experiences through her training as a designer (B.A, in Creative Direction, Design), Sound Meditation Facilitator, Yoga teacher and Shamanic Reiki practitioner. With a deep passion for holistic well-being, spiritual studies and designing intentional spaces, Cassie has spent the past three years immersing herself in a variety of spiritual, cultural, and traditional practices with many elders and wisdom-keepers around the world. She is working to merge the world of art, music and healing.
Adrian DiMatteo (B.M. Eastman School of Music, 2012) is the founder of the Sonic Institute. He is an international performing and recording artist, author ( Elements of Sound: A Full-Spectrum Exploration of Sound and Consciousness ), music educator, app creator ( Chord Atlas ), and sound meditation facilitator with over 20 years’ experience in the music world. Adrian has toured and traveled extensively—exploring sound, language, musical culture and how they affect individual and collective wellbeing. Hundreds of students have graduated from Sound Healer Training programs and weekend retreats that Adrian regularly facilitates nationwide. To date, he has offered musical experiences to thousands of people in hospitals, hospices, elder care facilities, prisons and children’s centers.
The Sonic Institute offers experiential workshops, music lessons, healing sessions, events and training designed to help people connect with sound and music as a tool for meditation and healing. Our programs unite ancient spirituality with modern science to reveal the impact of sound vibration on health and consciousness. Through online & in-person lessons, sound baths, presentations and interactive workshops, participants gain skills and knowledge needed to facilitate guided meditation and sound healing experiences.
