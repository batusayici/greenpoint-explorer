Membership
Our members are the heart of our community and the keepers of our space.
United by a love for wellness in its many forms, Held Space membership is for the person who is devoted to their practices and wants to experience meaningful connection with fellow Brooklynites.
Community Membership
Attend unlimited Members Only Events (at least 2x per week with offerings like yoga, ecstatic dance, sound baths, communal coworking, and more)
Access the common areas, including the lounge & quiet co-working space, from 7am-10pm, 7 days a week
Reserve the Gathering Room on demand at the discounted membership rate
Host non-profit and donation-based events for free
Get exclusive access to book the practice rooms on an hourly basis
Receive professionally designed marketing graphics + visibility on our social channels
Get personalized support bringing your ideas to life—from event design to promotion.
Unlock discounted rates with Held Space photographer & videographer
Join our members-only Slack channel: a place for networking, collaboration, and friendly conversation
$129/month or $1029/year (2 months free)
No minimum commitment.

Membership FAQs
Do I have to be a licensed professional to join the Collective?
Do I have to be a licensed professional to join the Collective?
No! Membership is open to anyone who shares our values. We do ask that you maintain your own liability insurance if you plan to host events or provide professional services at Held Space.
What are the values of the Collective?
Will I be employed by Held Space?
Will I be employed by Held Space?
No! If you’re running a business here, it remains 100% YOUR business. We provide the physical space & supportive resources to help your business thrive.
What are the values of the Collective?
Will Held Space manage my attendees?
Will Held Space manage my attendees?
Held Space does not manage attendee payment & registration for your events, but we’ll be happy to share your sign-up link on our website & newsletter!
What are the values of the Collective?
Does Held Space dictate ticket pricing or take a cut of my earnings?
Does Held Space dictate ticket pricing or take a cut of my earnings?
Nope! Your profits are yours to keep.
What are the values of the Collective?
Do I have to be a member to rent the Gathering Room?
Do I have to be a member to rent the Gathering Room?
No, but members receive priority booking & a significantly discounted rental rate.
