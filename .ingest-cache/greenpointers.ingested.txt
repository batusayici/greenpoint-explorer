## Lucky Creamery Scooping Up Asian-Inspired Ice Cream Flavors in Greenpoint
https://greenpointers.com/2026/08/05/lucky-creamery-scooping-up-asian-inspired-ice-cream-flavors-in-greenpoint/
Wed, 05 Aug 2026 13:00:00 +0000
Some of us are froyo fiends, and some of us (me) have dairy dessert tastes that skew elsewhere (namely, The Original Chinatown Ice Cream Factory). Luckily, there’s room for us all in the North Brooklyn area, with an additional ice cream place having just opened this past weekend.
Lucky Creamery quietly took over the storefront at 669 Manhattan Avenue, most recently home to Euro Chemist Pharmacy. Where the Screen Door (145 Driggs Ave.) keeps things classic and Caffé Panna (16 Norman Ave.) features an ever-changing menu, Lucky Creamery focuses on Asian-inspired flavors.
The store did not respond to our request for comment, but they recently shared a menu on Instagram stories. Scoops start at $6.50, with one topping and one drizzle. You also have the option of upgrading to a waffle cone ($7) or an egg waffle ($12.99, one scoop flavor, unlimited toppings and drizzles).
Flavor options include Earl Grey, black sesame, oolong white peach, lychee rose, and pandan, along with classics like vanilla, chocolate, and strawberry. Lucky Creamery also has vegan scoop options.
Aside from ice cream, Lucky Creamery offers smoothies, slushes, coffee, tea, and matcha drinks.
The store’s temporary open hours are Sunday through Thursday 12-10 pm, and weekends from 12-11pm.

## New Coffee Shop Loco Cafe to Open in Former Home of Viva La Pizza
https://greenpointers.com/2026/08/04/new-coffee-shop-loco-cafe-to-open-in-former-home-of-viva-la-pizza/
Tue, 04 Aug 2026 17:00:00 +0000
A new coffee shop is taking over the 500 square-foot space that was previously home to Viva La Pizza and Pizza Prince . Loco Cafe is aiming to open next week, but they told Greenpointers they do not have an exact date yet, but it “should be around the second week of August.”
Loco Cafe is the third eatery to call the storefront home in two years. After Pizza Prince closed in 2024, Viva La Pizza took its place for only a year and then closed.
Viva La Pizza’s Jose Colohua said that the space was not financially sustainable, echoing the same reason for Pizza Prince’s closure. The storefront was previously listed for a monthly rent of $15,000, though it’s unclear what Viva La Pizza actually paid.
Real estate agent Eric Lupo told Greenpointers that before Loco Cafe took over, the rent was listed $10,000 a month. He said that Loco Cafe signed a lease for $8,500 a month “with terms.”
While Pizza Prince and Viva La Pizza used the address of 86 Nassau Avenue, Loco Cafe is using the address 626 Manhattan Avenue. Lupo explained that the building contains the addresses 626 Manhattan Avenue and 82-86 Nassau Avenue, “since it’s a corner building.” Lupo confirmed that Loco Cafe rented the former pizzeria and that the deli located in the corner of the building remains open.
The exterior of Viva La Pizza, which opened in the former home of Pizza Prince in Greenpoint. Photo: Julia Moak
Loco Cafe told Greenpointers that the neighborhood’s newest coffee shop will be a specialty coffee shop focused on serving high-quality coffee in a “welcoming, community-driven space.”
“Our goal is to create a neighborhood café where people can enjoy carefully crafted espresso drinks, quality pastries, and simple, fresh breakfast options,” Loco Cafe said. “We’re putting a strong emphasis on hospitality, consistency, and making everyone who walks through the door feel at home.”
Loco Cafe called its space “cozy” and “Instagram-worthy.” They said that every detail is designed to create an “experience,” in addition to serving coffee.
“Whether you’re meeting friends, working, or just stopping by for your daily coffee, we want Loco Cafe to be your go-to spot,” the newcomer said.
