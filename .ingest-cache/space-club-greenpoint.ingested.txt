FREE CLASSES
Space Club offers free classes to our members at 10am and 11am every Monday - Friday
A Space Club membership is required to attend our free weekday classes. Classes are not included with Single Pass or ThreezyPass visits.
Classes are offered under the category of ART , SENSORY , MUSIC , and DANCE / MOVEMENT. See below for class schedule
They have featured everything from arts and crafts , singalongs , performances , dance parties , play doh sculpture , yoga through storytelling , children's book readings , even wig styling , and many, many more! They are primarily intended for children 1 - 5 but can be scalable enough to occasionally engage slightly older kids on school holidays. No need to book, just come on down!
