Greenpoint Library and Environmental Education Center serves as both a full-service library and a community hub for environmental awareness, activism and education.
At 15,000 square feet, the branch offers significant indoor and outdoor spaces for activities related to the exploration of the environment as well as everyday library use. Greenpoint Library features elements that interact with the natural world, including a gardened reading deck, windows that act as sun dials, and a cistern to collect rainwater for the rooftop demonstration garden. Inside, the library has distinct adult, young adult and children’s reading rooms and collection spaces, and community spaces for regular library programming as well as those supporting environmental education. Lab spaces for interactive projects, a large community event space (which divides into two of the lab spaces), lounge seating, small meeting rooms and staff spaces are distributed on the two main floors.
Free NARCAN® (naloxone) kits are available at Greenpoint Library
NARCAN® (naloxone) can reverse overdose from opioids. Pick up a free NARCAN® kit anonymously at Greenpoint Library. Trained staff will be on hand with instructions for safe use, including how to administer NARCAN® and help our community stay healthy. We also offer free Fentanyl and Xylazine testing strips.
Interested in learning more? Find BPL harm reduction programs.
North Brooklyn Environmental Community Calendar
Brooklyn Public Library’s North Brooklyn Environmental Community Calendar is a central hub for environmental events and initiatives in the neighborhood. The calendar features environmental programs hosted by Greenpoint Library as well as environmental events from local community partners and serves as a resource for residents to stay informed, connect with local organizations, and participate in local environmental efforts.
Friends of Greenpoint Library
Friends of Greenpoint Library is a group of organized volunteers who help our neighborhood library through advocacy, fundraising and promotion. Join the Friends of Greenpoint Library email list.
