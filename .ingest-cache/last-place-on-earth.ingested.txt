OPEN PLAY
Basic Guidelines:
It is $15 per adult to play for up to 2 hours. We do not have a rate that goes by the hour, nor do we lessen the rate depending on how long you plan to stay. We will come up to you with a 10 minute warning, encouraging you to wrap up your game OR extend your time!
We have a $25 day pass! No need to worry about rushing or not finishing your game. Relax and have an amazing time with the 1,100 choices we have in our space. If you are looking for a specific game, feel free to call us.
We only take reservations for 8 or more people and must be a payment in advance. Reservations should be made minimum 24 hours ahead of time. If you have less than 8 people, we most likely have room, so you can just walk in! If you want to feel extra secure, feel free to give us a call and see if there’s a waitlist.
Although we have snacks & beverages, we allow outside food & drinks to make sure your stay with us is as comfortable as possible! If you plan on bringing a lot, please take your garbage with you. - Any pour over drinks, such as wine, whiskey, is a $20 fee.
The front part of the shop has our games for sale, and the second half will have the game for play. Since the open play games is a personal collection, there’s a 50/50 chance we have it for sale on the sale side.
We have plenty of 2 player friendly games! In fact, we have a whole section dedicated to 2 player games. We are the perfect date spot.
Plus One & Only
Plus One & Only is our most-loved date night package, made for you and your favorite human. There's something magical about date night at Last Place—the ambient lighting, hundreds of games to explore, and a vibe so warm it practically hugs you. From first dates to long-term love, this $50 package sets the tone for a night you’ll both remember.
You’ll get two open play passes (good for a full two-hour reservation), a reserved table just for you, and a curated list of games tailored to your vibe—romantic, silly, strategic, competitive, cooperative, or something delightfully in between. We’ll set the scene with a tabletop lamp, your choice of a craft soda + tea pairing or a flavored seltzer, and a sweet or savory snack.
But here’s the magic touch: every Plus One & Only now comes with a mystery sticker surprise —a little memory to take home with you. You’re welcome to bring in outside food or slip across the street for a drink and come back for more play.
Once you email, we’ll send you a short form to personalize the night to your exact vibe. Last Place isn’t just a board game shop—it’s your cozy corner of the world. A home away from home where phones go down, laughter bubbles up, and the best moments unfold one card at a time. Reservations must be made 24hours in advance . Please wait for our response and for your advanced payment to guarantee your reservation.

Second location!
Last Place On Earth opened a second location called 3rd Place from the Sun. We opened September 15th, 2025 in Dumbo, Brooklyn. Our goal with this space is to expand the sense of community deeper into Brooklyn and hope it causes a positive ripple effect where we’re able to explore other boroughs! 3rd Place is what we call a “sister location” where it’s basically identical to the first location with a fun additional twist; this space has a private D&D room! Is pick up available for my child?
Our main schools that we pick up from every single day is PS 110, PS 132, PS 34, and PS 31 for no additional fee. If your child attends a school that is walking distance from us, such as St Stans and Cottonwood, we can sort something out.
when does it start & end?
Although we arrive with the children around 2:45 / 3pm, we begin at 3:30pm for the kids who are either coming on their own or coming from a far distance. This allows the kids who do arrive early to do homework or have some downtime for snacks and conversation. We end at 5:30pm. If you need to pick up your child early, we are flexible and can work to your schedule. We begin the first day of school and end on the last! Our first semester will run until the last day of January.

The Pokémon Club is a fantastical and vibrant program designed for young Pokémon enthusiasts eager to dive into the enchanting world of Pokémon. This club is a one-of-a-kind haven where imagination and passion for Pokémon come alive through a variety of engaging and interactive activities tailored for kids. Each session is a thrilling adventure, where members can explore different facets of the Pokémon universe, from trading cards and video games to animated series and lore. They will also delve into the rich history and stories behind their favorite Pokémon, enhancing their appreciation and understanding of this beloved franchise.
Beyond playing games, the Pokémon Club offers creative projects such as designing custom Pokémon, creating imaginative fan fiction, and even organizing mini-tournaments and themed events. These activities foster a sense of community and camaraderie as members share their unique interests and collaborate on exciting projects. The club also emphasizes the values of sportsmanship, strategy, and creativity, encouraging members to develop critical thinking and social skills in a fun and supportive environment.

acting 101
Ages 8 - 12 | 10 Seats Available
Instructor: Tim Leech
The Improv + Theater Club is an exciting and dynamic after-school program designed to nurture creativity, boost confidence, and enhance performance skills in students. Each week, participants will alternate between the high-energy, spontaneous world of improvisation and the structured, collaborative environment of theater. During improv weeks, students will engage in a variety of fun and challenging exercises that focus on quick thinking, teamwork, and developing the ability to create scenes and characters on the fly. These sessions will not only improve their acting skills but also build their confidence and communication abilities in a supportive, laughter-filled setting. On theater weeks, students will dive into the world of scripted plays and musicals, learning about character development, stage presence, and the intricacies of putting on a polished performance. They will work on scenes, monologues, and even participate in full-scale productions, gaining valuable experience both on stage and behind the scenes. The club aims to create a vibrant community where students can explore their passion for the performing arts, make new friends, and discover their unique voices. Whether they dream of starring in the next school play or simply want to try something new and fun, the Improv + Theater Club offers a welcoming space for all levels of experience.
