Kids Summer Camps
Our coaches have over 10 years of experience teaching kids circus and are committed to a fun, supportive learning environment. We have full day and half day options for 4-7 years old, 7-12 years old, and 13-17 years old.
Single Day
Our Single-Day Circus Camp is perfect for children who want to try camp before committing to a full session. Campers will explore a variety of circus arts, including aerial apparatuses such as lyra, silks, hammock, and dance trapeze, as well as ground activities like acro, juggling, and flow props. No prior experience is required. This is a great opportunity for children to try new skills and see if our circus camp is the right fit.
Two Days
Our Two-Day Circus Camp is a great option for families looking for a shorter camp experience. Campers will explore a variety of circus arts, including aerial apparatuses such as lyra, silks, hammock, and dance trapeze, as well as ground activities like acro, juggling, and flow props.
With two days to learn and practice, participants can build confidence, develop new skills, and enjoy an engaging introduction to circus arts. No prior experience is required.
Week Long
Our Week-Long Circus Camp offers a full, immersive experience for children to explore and develop their circus skills. Campers will learn a variety of circus arts, including aerial apparatuses such as lyra, silks, hammock, and dance trapeze, along with ground activities like acro, juggling, and flow props.
Throughout the week, participants will enjoy games, themed days, and opportunities to build skills and creativity. The camp concludes with a special showcase where campers perform for family and friends.
No prior experience is required.
