## BollyX
http://www.nycgovparks.org/events/2026/09/12/bollyx
Wed, 09 Sep 2026 00:00:02 EDT
BollyX is a Bollywood inspired cardio dance fitness class inspired and is driven by the heart-pumping, energetic rhythms of cultural music from all parts of India. The class embodies dynamic choreography and easy-to-follow dance aerobics.
