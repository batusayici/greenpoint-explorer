Eclipse season comes again in August, when we’ll experience the last of a series of eclipses in Pisces, and the first of a series of eclipses in Leo.
Eclipses are powerful solar and lunar events which have historically served as omens in many astrological traditions. Eclipses can correspond with periods of instability and change in the world, and can reflect major endings and beginnings in various sectors of our personal lives. In this workshop, we will practice ways to understand, respect, and respond to the shifts and upheavals that eclipses can represent.
This 2 hour workshop will include some lecture, some discussion, Q&A, and some exercises to practice with your own chart. I'll also provide some journaling prompts for reflection.
