Membership
Becoming a member
Because we put community at the center, we make it a point to meet all prospective members in person and introduce you to our space, our vision, and our values. Please don’t stop by without first applying, since we plan all tours in an organized fashion. In the meantime, get to know us a bit by reading our Community Agreements as you prepare to apply.
OUR PROCESS
Fill out our membership application so we can get to know you and your needs.
You will hear back from us to schedule a studio tour or direct you towards continuing your journey of learning*.
Tour Clay Space and get a feel for our community. Once we all agree, you’ll be offered a membership based on availability and interest.
*While we do value all skill levels, membership will only be considered for those with more than one year of consistent lessons and are able to commit to a 3-month minimum membership term. By filling out our membership application, this allows us to learn a bit about your skill level and needs. You will hear back from us within 10 days with a determination. If we feel you’re ready for a membership, we’ll invite you to schedule a studio tour so you can visit the space and get a feel for our community and amenities.
Our member’s work area does not include instruction, therefore all our memberships require a strong base level understanding of the entire ceramic process as well as several classes and/or practice experience that shows you’ve really grasped the process. This doesn’t mean you have to be a master ceramicist, but we do need to ensure you’re working at an independent and intermediate/advanced level. If we determine you are not quite ready for membership, take heart! This only means we think you’ll be better served by continuing with the support of an instructor’s guidance. Because we see how our students have developed their skills, they are in line for membership when spots open up before those from outside the community working at the same level. We recommend you consider one of our intermediate, advanced or independent study classes to expand your skills and experience and our instructors will help guide you towards membership.
For those that haven’t begun their clay journey or have some amount of experience, we would be honored to be a part of your learning and growth. We suggest starting with one of our many classes .
Amenities & Services for all membership levels
All members have 24/7 access to members work areas and equipment, kitchen with a refrigerator, microwave/air fryer, toaster oven, discounts on some Clay Space Workshops & Classes and inclusion in events such as our Annual Holiday Market & Open Studio events. View detailed amenity information .

Communal: work-table
Feel ready to grow beyond the classroom, practice your skills, and spend quality time with other makers? Is your work fairly small, you’re not in production, you want to get a feel for the studio? This communal membership is for you!
This tier includes shared use of potter’s wheels, 2 large communal handbuilding work tables, use of all equipment and all-level membership amenities.
Choose from either one or two shelves for your designated storage.
$210.00 - $255.00 + tax monthly
See the dimension and price
Semi Private: work-space
Do you need a work-space where you can let works in progress rest? Are you ready to dive deeper and invest more into your practice? Our semi-private locations in the studio will allow you the flexibility to work in your own space while still making alongside other creatives.
This tier includes a designated work table and pegboard divider, additional storage under the table & may include either wall or side shelving based on your particular membership level.
With this level, you are eligible for being included in bulk supply orders with our clay vendors and opportunity to schedule your own kiln.
$310 - $380 + tax monthly
See the dimension and price
Semi-Private & Private: work-annex
Are you a small ceramic business that wants to be a part of the Clay Space community but need extra space for your production and/or slip casting work? Do you like a bit more privacy but remain a part of the community? If so, the work-annex membership is for you!
The Clay Space Annex is a 700 sq. ft. ground floor, private entrance studio space located just across the street from our center. You will be sharing the space with 12 other members.
This tier includes both semi private & private options along with full access to Clay Space Ceramic Center and all of its amenities and equipment.
Annex amenities:
Communal Hand building tables
(1) 7 cu. ft. kiln & (1) 2.6 cu. ft. test kiln
Norstar Slab roller
Norstar Extruder
(4) Potter’s wheels
Utility sink
Refrigerator and counter
Wifi Access
$365 - $525 + tax monthly
See the dimension and price
Private: work-studio
Do you need space and time to hone your craft but also like to be amongst other clay folks? This membership tier provides you with lots of storage space. It’s private, but never lonely.
This tier includes a designated work table & pegboard divider, storage under the table & a large private shelving unit adjacent to your table. This is the perfect size for a production potter or someone who makes large work.
You are eligible for being included in bulk supply orders with our clay vendors and opportunity to schedule your own kiln.
$485 - $650 + tax monthly
