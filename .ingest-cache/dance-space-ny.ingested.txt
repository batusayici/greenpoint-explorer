Adult Ballet Mondays 6:45-8pm
Open level- all are welcome!
Adult Beginner Tap Tuesdays 6:30-7:30pm
Great for total beginners who want to try something new! No shoes required to start- try it in sneakers!
Adult Intermediate Tap Thursdays 6:30-7:30pm
Great for dancers who have taken beginner tap and feel ready for a new challenge, or dancers who used to tap and are returning to it!
Adult Beginner Jazz Thursdays 7:30-8:30pm
Sassy, fun, and energetic! Great for beginners or returners! Jazz technique- any shoes are fine!
Adult Sneaker Jazz (Pro style dance) Fridays 7-8:30pm
Intermediate/Advanced class- for dancers planning to audition for pro teams, or dancers who want to revisit their love or dance team style choreography
