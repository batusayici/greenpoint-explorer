Luxury Tea Tastings
After much anticipation, Bellocq Tea Atelier would like to personally invite you to join us for an intimate focused tasting session in our Greenpoint, Brooklyn atelier. Across an array of countries, terrain and cultivars, guests will be introduced to a broad and dazzling spectrum of tea and together, we contemplate their rich and varied nuance while tracing the remarkable journey of water from deep, ancient aquifers and terroir, to the celebrated leaf in your cup.
