Sunday Gardening - 2026 - Circularity in the Landscape: reuse in our gardens and parks
Sunday, September 27, 2026
10:00 AM
12:00 PM
WNYC Transmitter Park
Brooklyn, NY 11222
(map)
Google Calendar
ICS
Join us on select Sunday mornings as we roll up our sleeves and care for WNYC Transmitter Park’s gardens! Today we’ll be working on a pathway in the pollinator garden. Rosemarie Miner, from Our Temenos will discuss circularity practices based on re-use of building materials in our gardens and parks.
What to Bring: Gardening gloves (if you have them), a hat, sunscreen, and water. Don’t worry if you don’t have gloves—we’ve got extras!
What to Expect: A morning of hands-on work, meeting neighbors and volunteers from across the city, and making a real impact in the park.
Where to Meet: Just inside the Greenpoint Ave entrance.
Sign up here
Registering helps us track volunteer hours and ensure everyone has the right supplies. See you in the garden!
