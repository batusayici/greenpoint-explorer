Located alongside the McCarren Park track and dog run, the McCarren Park Greenmarket has connected local farms and farmers to the residents of the Greenpoint and Williamsburg neighborhoods since 1997. Every Saturday, year-round, families, long-time residents and young couples can find a diverse selection of locally-grown and produced foods throughout the market. Beyond shopping, visitors can enjoy cooking demonstrations, educational programs, and activities for families, creating an interactive and welcoming experience for all ages.
