PLAY KIDS Summer Camp 2026
For children ages 3 -4 & 4-8 years old at PL AY Kids Greenpoint
Proudly voted “Best After‑School & Camp Program” in Brooklyn.
For more than a decade, PLAY Kids Summer Camp has been the neighborhood’s home for adventurous, joy‑filled summers. Year after year, we welcome returning campers—and their equally delighted parents—back into our safe, nurturing haven. Our mission is clear: to deliver an exceptional, enriching experience where children can explore, play, and build lifelong friendships, leaving them eager to come back each summer.
PLAY Kids Summer Camp is a full‑day program uniquely balanced between outdoor exploration and indoor creativity. Set against the vibrant backdrop of McCarren Park, our camp offers daily drop‑off services for busy families and a rich lineup of activities designed to cultivate well‑rounded campers. From hands‑on science and nature‑inspired art to spirited soccer and basketball games—and even playful French lessons—every day brings fresh challenges and unforgettable memories.
