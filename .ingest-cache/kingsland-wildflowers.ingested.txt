The 10th Annual Kingsland Wildflowers Festival returns Saturday, August 1! This free, family-friendly event will include exciting activities and the chance to:
Explore our rooftop wildflower meadows 🪻
Experience art installations, performances, & live music 🎶
Learn about native wildlife & ecology, including Newtown Creek 🐟
Meet local community & environmental organizations 💚
Buy native plants for your garden 🌼
Enjoy food & drinks with friends & family 🥗
RSVP for free at KWFestival2026.eventbrite.com !
Kingsland Wildflowers is a 25,000 sqft. native pollinator garden nestled in Greenpoint, Brooklyn’s industrial business zone. The project partners include: Alive Structures , Broadway Stages , Newtown Creek Alliance , and Noo Arts .

Come join us for Open Hours at Kingsland Wildflowers — learn about the green roofs and native plants while enjoying a scenic view!
Open Hours at Kingsland Wildflowers — an opportunity for the public to learn about the green roofs, the native plants present, and the Newtown Creek while enjoying the golden hour from our scenic wildflower meadows. Open hours are free and open to the public and will take place every Friday from April 24 to June 26, 2026, 5-8 PM weather-permitting.
For the 2026 Open Hours season, our Newtown Native Nursery will be open, allowing visitors to explore (and purchase) native plants that we grow on site, which can be a great addition for any greenspace you may have or be involved in!
This is a family friendly event, kindly keep in mind that we use our green roofs as an educational resource, we prefer that furry friends stay at home. NCA staff will be on hand to answer questions about the space, the ecological benefits of green roofs, and talk about our current activities around Newtown Creek! We will have drinks and light refreshments on hand for guests to enjoy.
Music and additional proggramming on select Fridays:
April 24th - 5:30 p.m. Drifter & 6:30 p.m. Joe Feldmann
May 8th - Melting Metropolis ' storyteller in residence Isa Dorvillier
May 15th - Traditional Irish Filk Music
June 19th - Berenice Suchilt
June 26th - Maidenhead + Aaron Roche
Please note that not all roofs are ADA compliant, our paths have a 34” width and can have some uneven surfaces including bluestone, small gravel, and soil. For more information about access at Kingsland Wildflowers visit our website .
Registrants will be notified of any cancellations on the day of the event. For other volunteer opportunities with Newtown Creek Alliance visit our events page .
---------
Kingsland Wildflowers is a joint project among Newtown Creek Alliance, Broadway Stages, Alive Structures, and Noo Arts.

Come join us for Open Hours at Kingsland Wildflowers - an opportunity for the public to learn about the green roofs, the native plants present, and the Newtown Creek while enjoying the golden hour from our scenic wildflower meadows. Open hours are free and open to the public and will take place every Friday from April 18th to June 27th, 2025, weather-permitting.
During 2025 Open Hours season, we will have out Newtown Native Nursery up and running, allowing visitors to explore (and purchase) native plants that we grow on site.
This is a family friendly event, kindly keep in mind that we use our green roofs as an educational resource, we prefer that furry friends stay at home. NCA staff will be on hand to answer questions about the space, the ecological benefits of green roofs, and talk about our current activities around Newtown Creek! We will have drinks and light refreshments on hand for guests to enjoy.

Greenhouse Gang is back! Join us April 15th as we kick off Spring with our first hands-on event!
NCA welcomes volunteers and horticulture enthusiasts to help us expand our native nursery, propagated from locally collected wild seed. Plants grown in our greenhouse are used to supplement our restoration projects around the Creek, maintain the rooftop meadows at Kingsland Wildflowers, and integrated into our Newtown Native Nursery . This is an ideal opportunity for those looking to support an attractive habitat for our local ecology.
Our horticulturist Brenda Suchilt will be on site to help guide activities such as up-potting and plant maintenance, as well as answer any questions you may have.
Following our kick-off event, the series will then run every third Tuesday of the month throughout the growing season. When selecting a ticket type, please register for a specific date to volunteer with the Greenhouse Gang you are expected to join.
