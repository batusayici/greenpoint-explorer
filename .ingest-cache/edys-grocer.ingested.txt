title: Dinner at the Grocer with Maria Koutsogiannis
excerpt: It’s time for our September Dinner at the Grocer! We’re so excited to welcome Maria Koutsogiannis, recipe developer, creator, and author of the new cookbook Greek Out , for a special Greek x Lebanese Dinner at the Grocer! 🇬🇷🇱🇧 We’re coming together to celebrate Greek Out and we’re really bringing the cookbook to life with a whole menu from the book; marrying Greek and Lebanese flavors to one table. And of course, we’ll be ending the evening with something very special! Homemade Chocolate Olive Oil Ice Cream with raspberry swirl topped with labneh mousse and served with a Greek version of biscuit au chocolate filled with nuts! Maria is coming all the way from Seattle for this one night only dinner!
