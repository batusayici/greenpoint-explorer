Echoes of the Archive
Fri 09.18.2026
7:00PM
-

Echoes of the Archive is returning to L&SD September 18th!! Join 4 DJs electronically reimagining traditional archival and diasporic music. Blending historic recordings from Beirut to Cochabamba, these artists will present dreamy, dubbed-out textures, creating a space where old voices speak in new forms.
Opening the night is a Yiddish-inspired somatic movement session by Sunny Love, scored by Chaia. Sunny Love guides an embodied exploration of gesture, sensation, memory and rhythm, inviting the body to become a living archive that is continually remembering, transforming and being rewritten.
The night will continue with a beautifully meditative performance from Roland Santana. Roland blends stretched-out field recordings with slow, tectonic bass, creating an ambient soundscape that moves between haze and rhythm. For this set, he revisits sounds collected during a recent trip to Cochabamba, Bolivia—a place connected to his heritage and a chance to reconnect with that part of himself. Street sounds, nature, and traditional folk instruments are broken apart and reshaped into something both unfamiliar and familiar. Using tools like a geophone and shotgun mic, Roland pushes these recordings into new territory while holding onto their original character..
Next, we’re excited to feature a DEBUT collaborative performance from Omar Ahmad and Solpara. Presenting their signature sounds live together for the first time ever, Omar Ahmad & Solpara will infuse their electronic textures with Arabic percussion, clapping, improv and much more!
Closing the night will be a performance by Chaia featuring sounds from Yiddish archives that explore historic anti-Zionism, collective memory, and radical political possibilities for utopian futures.

A requiem for the end of the world. Elori Saxl & Henry Solomon (LIVE), Ben Bondy (DJ), San Huan (LIVE), UCC Harlo (LIVE), and Marble Rye (DJ). Sweeping strings, a lone whistle on the wind, the hum of something vast and hungry drawing near but just out of reach. A tumbleweed rolls through, dragging time in its tangle. Heat rises off the asphalt in waves, before the ground goes liquid, a subaquatic bass rumbles beneath, air turning damp like breath on your neck, until consciousness transmutes into the shimmer and we all become one mirage. Set fire to your aura and dance on its ashes.
Elori Saxl & Henry Solomon
Elori Saxl is a composer whose music aches on a vast scale, haunting, cinematic, and forever reaching for something just past the horizon. Together with saxophonist Henry Solomon, they weaves vapor and brass into a stirring, elegiac expanse on their album Seeing Is Forgetting.
Ben Bondy
Ben Bondy is a Brooklyn producer from the West Mineral and 3XL orbit. He makes warm, dub-laced ambient and tin sound design that drifts between quiet introspection and the edge of the club.
San Huan
San Huan is a Brooklyn producer and live performer who shapes dub, IDM, and techno into intimate, uncanny things, built from physical gesture and voice-like synthesis.
UCC Harlo
UCC Harlo is Annie Garlid, a classically trained violist and singer who samples her own medieval and baroque playing into something she calls ecclesiastical-wave, where old music haunts the new.
Marble Rye
Marble Rye is the resident and curator behind Good Grief and one half of Mess Is Lore. An unpredictable selector, he spins broken rhythms, drone, and new-age haze into long hypnotic descents that bend time and rearrange the room.

Arto Lindsay has stood at the intersection of music and art for more than four decades. As a member of DNA, he contributed to the foundation of No Wave. As bandleader for the Ambitious Lovers he developed an intensely subversive pop music,a hybrid of American and Brazilian styles. Throughout his career, Lindsay has collaborated with both visual and musical artists, including Vito Acconci, Laurie Anderson, Animal Collective, Matthew Barney, Caetano Veloso and Rirkrit Tiravanija.
Melvin Gibbs has been called "the best bassist in the world" by Time Out New York magazine. A well-respected veteran of the New York music scene, he has contributed his singular talents to major artists in many diverse genres of music, appearing on close to 200 albums.
Dr. S+M is the project of composer, producer and L&SD studio archivist, Dan Rosato. Incorporating elements of dance music, field recordings and free improvisation to create sonic worlds that shift like the weather, each performance is a ritual of emergence and an exercise in focused communal listening. (dr s+m bandcamp)
Beat Detectives is the ongoing collaborative studio practices of Chris Hontos (HONTOS, Dreamweapon, Food Pyramid, Night Court) and Aaron Anderson. Their lush, trippy lo-fi rhythms playfully blend influences such as '80s Chicago house, new wave, dub, and boogie.
Exoplex uses software to blur the line between organic and synthetic sound, drawing inspiration from microbiology, altered states, and parallel processing.
Stewey Decimal (aka Alex Mallis) is a filmmaker and DJ. His co-directed the feature film THE TRAVEL COMPANION, which premiered at Tribeca and will be distributed later this year on the Criterion Channel. His dj trio PLEASURE JAMS has been selecting dance music across New York for the better part of a decade.
Join us for a night of free-range sonic tomfoolery and community. Dinner will be available for purchase.

Biking to Rockaway
Sun 09.20.2026
8:00AM
-
1:00PM
Join us for a 30-mile bike tour along the Brooklyn Greenway, from WNYC Transmitter Park in Greenpoint to the Rockaway Amphitheater!
The ride is 3 hours of active biking, with access to restrooms/water at multiple rest stops.
Our last stop before crossing Jamaica Bay is the Tashkent Supermarket, where we'll pause for a communal meal!
Exact departure time TBD but will likely be early-ish in morning so we can catch a morning breeze, ride more casual, and not be rushed at the rest stops.
The aim is for people to enjoy a solid window for a swim/hang at a beach of their preference before we join the LastSunDay party at the Amphitheater.
Bikes are allowed on NYC subways & ferries for those wanting to take the transit back home!
