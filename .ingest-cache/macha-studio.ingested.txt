## Oona Ratcliffe
https://machastudio.com/blogs/events/art-by-oona-ratcliffe
2024-03-06T17:03:24-05:00
We are delighted to present the art of local artist Oona Ratcliffe at Macha Studio throughout March.
Ratcliffe’s paintings, seductive frenzies of color and neon line, are constructed in a framework of loose sensuality. Tending towards the acidic and artificial, saturated color is offset by impenetrable flatness, lyrical line deceived by c areful manipulation, intricate image belied by an absence of modeling or brush- stroke. Ratcliffe’s work is a play of opposites—an endless search for frozen stillness in a world of constant movement and an inherent tension between outside and inside, trans parency and opaci ty, nature and urbanism.
Oona Ratcliffe , b . 1975 in Bolinas, C A , received an MFA from Hunter College C ity University of New York and also studied at the Ecole de Louvre Paris, France and the Universitas Gadjah Madad Yogyaka rta, Indonesia. Ratcliffe has exhibited her work in exhibitions across the United States including solo exhibitions at The Bolinas M useum , Susan Inglet Gallery and Gallery nine 5 and group shows at Morgan Lehman Gallery, Jeff Bailey Gallery, David Nolan Gallery, the Bolinas Museum, Roberts and Tilton Gallery and Geoffrey Young Gallery . She was awarded a Janet Sloan Yaddo though Yaddo, Saratoga , N Y . Ratcliffe currently lives and works in Brooklyn, N Y .

## Poetry Sessions
https://machastudio.com/blogs/events/poetry-nights
2023-01-01T16:30:00-05:00
Our Open Mic Poetry Nights are held once a month in our Greenpoint shop. They began on the summer of 2022 with the main purpose of bringing people together, creating a space where everyone is welcome to share and express a bit of their hearts.
Poetry is like a bridge. A bridge that connects the sacred with what matters. Reconnecting us with beauty, meaning & joy.
Poetry is an immediate path to becoming better and creating a change. Creating a world less about tearing each other down and apart, and more about coming together. Our poetry events allow a safe space to share, heal and express from the depth of our hearts and with it connect with others.
