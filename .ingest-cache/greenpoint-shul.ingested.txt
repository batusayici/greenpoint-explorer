4:30PM
Tashlich at Transmitter Park
Join friends, neighbors, and loved ones from across North Brooklyn for our annual Community-Wide Tashlich at Transmitter Park. Tashlich (תשליך) is the practice of symbolically casting away our missteps into flowing water as we begin the new year. Together, we will reflect on the justice and compassion we hope to manifest for humanity, and celebrate the sweetness that already fills our lives. Rooted in tradition and open to all, this gathering is a beautiful way to step into 5787 side-by-side.
