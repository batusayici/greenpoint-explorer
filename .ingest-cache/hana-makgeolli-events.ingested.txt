GYOPO BREWERY x HANA MAKGEOLLI
Sunday, July 26, 2026
5:00 PM
10:00 PM
HANA MAKGEOLLI
(map)
Google Calendar
ICS
Gyopo (교포): a term for those who leave their homeland, build a life abroad, and carry its roots with them.
Based in Toronto, Gyopo Makgeolli Brewery is a new kind of Korean brewpub — modern, unmistakably hip, and centered on house-brewed makgeolli served through a dedicated draft system. Our menu reimagines Korean cooking with technique and perspective drawn from beyond Korea, offering flavors that feel familiar yet arrive somewhere unexpected.
On July 26th, for one day only, we bring that philosophy to Brooklyn. Chef Hansang Lee will present Gyopo's menu at Hana Makgeolli, with each dish carefully paired to a Hana makgeolli — a collaboration between two gyopo (Hansang Lee X Alice Jeon), two cities, and one shared meal.
Join us for a menu built for one night only.

SUMMER RELEASE
The Hana Sool Club summer release features first access to our newest expression, KINGKANGJU, a co-ferment of rice and kumquat, alongside our flagship TAKJU 16. Each showcases the bright, expressive character of Korean rice wine and the depth that emerges through careful fermentation.
KINGKANGJU (New Release) is the second in our line of fruit coferments. Kingkangju is an unfiltered rice wine and a coferment of rice with kumquats. Made over five months from harvest to bottle, KINGKANGJU reflects the patience and seasonality at the heart of Hana’s brewing practice. At 10% ABV, its bright acidity, fruit-forward character, and refreshing profile, it strikes a balance between the structure and depth of rice and the lifted, vibrant character of fresh fruit.
TAKJU 16 is our flagship unfiltered rice wine, showcasing the depth and complexity of jeongtongju: traditionally made Korean alcohol.
4 Bottle Membership
KINGKANGJU - 750 ml | 10% ABV, kumquat and rice co-ferment. Still, sedimented, tart, lightly sweet, and lively with the vivid acidity of kumquat and the perfume of its zest
TAKJU 16 - 750 ml | 16% ABV, sedimented, heavy body, near-dry, with floral notes of melon and pineapple
HYUNMI TAKJU - 750 ml | 16% ABV, sedimented, medium body, earthy with a savory, warm chestnut profile from the sweet brown rice
YAKJU 14 - 750 ml | 14% ABV, clarified, light body, dry, refined, bright with naturally occurring lactic acid
