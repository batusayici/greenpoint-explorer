GYOPO BREWERY x HANA MAKGEOLLI
Sunday, July 26, 2026
5:00 PM
10:00 PM
HANA MAKGEOLLI
(map)
Google Calendar
ICS
Gyopo (교포): a term for those who leave their homeland, build a life abroad, and carry its roots with them.
Based in Toronto, Gyopo Makgeolli Brewery is a new kind of Korean brewpub — modern, unmistakably hip, and centered on house-brewed makgeolli served through a dedicated draft system. Our menu reimagines Korean cooking with technique and perspective drawn from beyond Korea, offering flavors that feel familiar yet arrive somewhere unexpected.
On July 26th, for one day only, we bring that philosophy to Brooklyn. Chef Hansang Lee will present Gyopo's menu at Hana Makgeolli, with each dish carefully paired to a Hana makgeolli — a collaboration between two gyopo (Hansang Lee X Alice Jeon), two cities, and one shared meal.
Join us for a menu built for one night only.

6
JILLI x HANA MAKGEOLLI
Saturday, June 6, 2026
2:00 PM
11:00 PM
HANA MAKGEOLLI
(map)
Google Calendar
ICS
For years, Jilli LA and Jilli SF have been some of Hana Makgeolli's biggest supporters, pouring our makgeolli with so much care and introducing Korean sool to communities on the West Coast. On Saturday, June 6, they’re bringing their beloved sooljib concept to NYC for the very first time.
Expect a special menu from the Jilli team including perilla oil noodles, rigatoni alla kimchi vodka, seasonal hwe, galbi frites, and the debut of their new Jilli kimbap alongside Hana Makgeolli, Hana Soju, and a room full of good people.
We’re so excited to welcome the Jilli team into our home for a night that feels deeply full circle!
Saturday 6/6 2 PM until late Hana Makgeolli Brewery & Tasting Room 201 Dupont St, Brooklyn
