Interested in Membership?
Membership at Yaro is intended for ceramicists who are comfortable working independently and have a solid understanding of the full ceramic process.
To be considered for membership, please complete our Membership Application. We'll review your application and follow up by email.
If approved, we'll invite you to visit the studio and discuss membership options, availability, and next steps.
Not quite ready for membership?
No worries! Many members begin through our classes, workshops, or independent study offerings before transitioning into membership.
Questions? Email us at info@yarostudios.com
Apply For Membership
Membership includes:
24/7 access
shelf storage
access to studio facilities and all ceramic equipment
throwing wheels with personal work stations
communal work tables & tools
20+ glazes
Discounts on yaro workshops & classes
Opportunities to collaborate in upcoming curated shows
A little about the space:
Prime & accessible location on Kent St in Greenpoint, Brooklyn (a block away from the Greenpoint Av G stop and 2 blocks from the East River Ferry)
Natural sunlight & state of the art studio lighting
Fully air conditioned & heated studio with HVAC & filtration system
Throwing wheels & throwing designated areas
5 Kilns, all available for rental
Large communal work tables for hand-building
Glazing room, glazing equipment & studio test tiles
Quiet work environment
Pricing
$300/mo Full Shelf & $260/mo Half Shelf
*Membership pricing includes tax. Additional costs to our membership fees include clay and firing fees at 4 cents per cubic inch bisque/glaze firings & tax.
Commitment & Cancellation Notice
Memberships require a minimum 3-month commitment. After that, you can cancel anytime with at least 30 days’ notice before your next billing date.
Please email info@yarostudios.com for questions :)
