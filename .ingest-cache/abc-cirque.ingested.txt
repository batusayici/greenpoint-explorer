Family Adventure Play
Ages 5 & Under with Adult
all sessions will be hosted at 5th Wall Studios in Williamsburg, Brooklyn
Circus Adventure Play is a child-led, exploration-based experience designed especially for children under five and their grown-ups. Together, parents and children build strength, flexibility, coordination, and trust while discovering the exciting world of aerial arts in a safe, thoughtfully designed environment.
At this age, children need opportunities to take healthy risks in spaces where hazards are carefully managed. Our knowledgeable staff creates a supportive play setting where little ones can climb, hang, swing, balance, and explore at their own pace — with caregivers close by. This kind of adventure play is essential for early childhood development, supporting motor skills, body awareness, confidence, resilience, and problem-solving.
Through guided but largely self-directed play, children and adults learn to communicate both verbally and nonverbally while navigating circus elements together. The focus is not on performance or perfection, but on exploration, connection, and joy.
This class is designed for caregivers and children to participate together — no unaccompanied children, please.
For children ages 5 and over, we offer structured instructional classes and private lessons. However, ABC Cirque is a strong advocate for self-directed play as a powerful foundation for lifelong learning.
Come climb, swing, explore — and grow together.

General Class Policies

If you need to cancel your registration, you must do so 48 hours prior to the start of the session or the charge will not be refundable. We are very strict about this policy and no exceptions will be made (including illness and weather) as it affects scheduling and payroll.
All Semesters, Rental Deposits and Workshops are Non-Transferrable and Non-Refundable.
Dress Code
Updated 2024
Circus training has very specific safety parameters unique from other movement arts. Please adhere to the dress code and know the coaches have the right to refuse training if wardrobe endangers students or apparatuses.
No zippers, buttons, watches, and jewelry are permitted. Wear fitted and footless athletic leggings that cover and protect the backs of the knees. Long hair needs to be tied up. No long artificial nails: these nails limit the person's ability to grip or safely stay in the air or maintain proper hand placement during ground acrobatic moves
For beginner classes only : fitted, basic tank tops or tee shirts covering midriffs are permitted.
For open level and advanced classes: unitards or leotards are best to cover midriffs to protect skin.
If you have any questions please check with your coach or email info@abcirque.com
Privacy
When registering for a class, workshop or special event, you will be asked to provide us with an email address and phone number. We may use the contact information you have given to us to notify you about important changes to our schedule and special offers in regards to the studio. If you would rather not receive this or any other information, simply click on "unsubscribe from this list" from the link provided at the bottom of our weekly e-mail at any time. We will honor your request. If you cannot find the link, please email us at info@abcirque.com and request not to receive our weekly emails.
We collect payment information when you register and/or pay for services and products provided by the Company. Information collected online is used exclusively to make payment for the service or product specified and is not used or retained for any other purpose. goes here
Substance
ABCirque does not tolerate the use of illegal drugs or alcohol in or around the studio during classes and workshops. If you are not sober when you show up to class, you will not be allowed to participate and no refund will be issued. Alcoholic beverages may be permitted in conjunction with special events but no one will be allowed to participate in aerial activities if they have consumed alcohol.
