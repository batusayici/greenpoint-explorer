The Battle of Brooklyn Tour
Thursday, August 13th at 2:00 PM - 3:00 PM
Free
&laquo; Tot Time! (MPJ State Park)
Sewing Class Workshops (Williamsburgh Library) &raquo;
Join Prospect Park Alliance for a free tour of the park to learn about the Battle of Brooklyn, the first and largest battle of the American War for Independence, on its 250th Anniversary. This one-hour tour begins and ends at Lefferts Historic House, and takes visitors to the park’s Battle Pass to see the replica fortifications where on August 27, 1776, thousands of Pennsylvania and New England soldiers fought their British and Hessian counterparts. Along the way, attendees will learn about the battle and its aftermath from the perspective of the Africans enslaved by the Lefferts family, soldiers who fought in what is now Prospect Park, and the overall impact of the conflict on Flatbush and Brooklyn. Please note this tour leaves promptly at 2 pm.
Also enjoy our exhibit about the important and varied roles Black people played in the Battle of Brooklyn, on view in the Lefferts yard.
Learn More + RSVP .
.
Dates: daily through August 27, 2026
CANCELLED! Wednesday, July 15
CANCELLED! Mondays & Tuesdays from August 17 to August 25
Please Note: To ensure a safe and comfortable experience for everyone, tours may be canceled in the event of extreme heat or heavy rain. We recommend checking the Prospect Alliance website or social media channels for real-time weather updates before heading out to the park.

Brooklyn Bridge Park Movies With A View
Thursday, August 13th at 6:00 PM - 11:00 PM
Event Series
(See All)
Free
&laquo; Kayaking in Brooklyn Bridge Park
Movies Under the Stars (Aug 2026) &raquo;
Brooklyn Bridge Park’s free outdoor film series, Movies With A View presented by Persol, is back for 2026! Enjoy free screenings on Thursday evenings in July and August – expanding with an additional family film night on Friday, September 18.
This summer’s lineup features beloved classics and modern hits, taking place across the globe from Italy to Costa Rica and back to New York City. With a diverse blend of genres, including action, romantic comedy, and a musical, there is something for everyone to enjoy.
Each evening, the lawn opens at 6:00 PM and the movie begins at sunset. Entry is free on a first-come, first-served basis.
.
Movie line-up
CANCELLED! Thursday, July 2: Hamilton (PG-13, 2020, 160 minutes) – Kick off the 4th of July weekend with New York’s own Lin-Manuel Miranda’s imaginative retelling of American founding father Alexander Hamilton. This is the filmed version of the Broadway production – singing along is encouraged!
Thursday, July 9: Hamilton (PG-13, 2020, 160 minutes) – Kick off the 4th of July weekend with New York’s own Lin-Manuel Miranda’s imaginative retelling of American founding father Alexander Hamilton. This is the filmed version of the Broadway production – singing along is encouraged!
Thursday, July 16: Bend it like Beckham (PG-13, 2002, 112 minutes) – Leading up to the World Cup Final, revisit the popular film about a young woman chasing her passion for soccer despite her family’s wishes.
Thursday, July 23: The Talented Mr. Ripley (R, 1999, 139 minutes) – Based on the bestselling novel, this psychological thriller sets Matt Damon, Jude Law, and Gwyneth Paltrow against the Italian coast, making it the perfect film to watch on a summer evening in the park.
Thursday, July 30: Clueless (PG-13, 1995, 97 minutes) – The beloved film adapted from Jane Austen’s Emma about a high school student playing matchmaker for her peers and teachers is a perfect movie to watch on the lawn with friends.
Thursday, August 6: Jurassic Park (PG-13, 1993, 122 minutes) – The first film of the epic franchise thirty years in the making, revisit the original movie when scientists first set foot on Isla Nubar and encounter the adventure of a lifetime.
Thursday, August 13: Do the Right Thing (R, 1989, 120 minutes) – Directed by Brooklyn’s Spike Lee, this classic film follows the stories of various Bed-Stuy residents on a hot summer day, culminating in violence and tragedy.
Thursday, August 20: Bridesmaids (R, 2011, 125 minutes) – This box-office hit comedy features career-defining performances by Kristen Wiig, Maya Rudolph, Rose Byrne, Melissa McCarthy and more.
Thursday, August 27: Public Choic e – In early August, the public gets to vote for their favorite selection from three options to view on August 27! Selections include Challengers (R, 2024, 131 minutes), The Great Gatsby (PG-13, 2013, 143 minutes), and Ocean’s Eleven (PG-13, 2001, 116 minutes). Voting takes place online at brooklynbridgepark.org.
Friday, September 18: How to Train Your Dragon (PG, 2010, 93 minutes) – Extending to our first September viewing, join for family film night with this fun animated story about a young Viking who befriends a dragon.
.
This year’s line-up of free screenings is presented by Persol. Throughout the series, Persol will bring the spirit of the Italian Riviera to Brooklyn Bridge Park through immersive on-site activations inspired by the effortless elegance, and cinematic atmosphere of the brand’s Spring/Summer 2026 campaign and collection.
Guests are encouraged to arrive early to grab a spot at Pier 1 Harbor View Lawn in Brooklyn Bridge. Guests are welcome to bring their own food for picnicking and encouraged to support nearby Park concessions and neighborhood restaurants. Popcorn, water, soft drinks, and alcoholic beverages (for those 21+) will be available for sale on-site by Fornino. Attendees are encouraged to bring picnic blankets or small soft cushions but reminded that lawn chairs and dogs are not permitted on the lawn, and glass bottles and outside alcohol are never allowed in the Park. For full details, please visit brooklynbridgepark.org/event-series/movies-with-a-view

Thursday, August 13th at 5:00 PM - 7:00 PM
Free
&laquo; Sewing Class Workshops (Williamsburgh Library)
History Tours (Domino Park) &raquo;
Glide along the water this summer and fall in our protected embayment with free public kayaking!
Presented in partnership with Brooklyn Bridge Park Boathouse, free kayaking is offered throughout the summer on Wednesday and Thursday evenings, Saturdays, and Sunday afternoons. All levels are welcome including first-time kayakers — life vests and all equipment provided, as well as instruction. Children under 18 must have an adult guardian present. Meet at the north entrance of Pier 2, look for the floating dock.
Advanced registration required, providing 20 minute timed slots. Free registration opens 2 weeks ahead of each date through the Brooklyn Bridge Park Boathouse website. View all kayaking dates here. Please note that holiday weekends may be skipped; please check the schedule.
Register here!
Don’t see any available time slots? Check back! Registration opens on a rolling basis, 2 weeks ahead of each date. Occasionally, slots may open due to cancellations.
Have your own boat? Launch your human-powered boats from the Pier 4 Beach. Learn more.
.
